**Description (4000):**

```
Haddington Bible is the Self-Interpreting Bible of John Brown of Haddington, on your phone.

Brown first published his Bible at Edinburgh in 1778. Beside each verse, he put references to other passages that explain it. After each chapter, he added notes and reflections. The name comes from the rule that Scripture is its own best interpreter.

WHAT YOU CAN READ
• The King James text of each book of the Bible
• The margin references under each verse
• Brown's notes and reflections after each chapter
• The notes of Henry Cooke and Josiah L. Porter from the four-volume edition
• The books of the Apocrypha with their margin references, in a separate section

HOW IT WORKS
• Tap a reference to read the passage in the King James Version
• Tap a verse number to go to Brown's note on that verse
• Memorise a verse in Hearers, or sing a psalm in Precentor
• Choose the text size, and turn the margin references and the modern measures on or off
• Light and dark follow the setting of your device

The transcription comes from scans of the printed pages. A few chapters do not have their notes yet. The contents show which chapters have notes.

PRIVATE
Haddington Bible has no account, no advertisements and no tracking. Your settings stay on your device. All the text is in the app, so it works with no connection.

FREE
Haddington Bible is free. The printed book is in the public domain. The website is haddingtonbible.org.
```

**What's new (version 1):**

```
The first release on the App Store.
```

**App Privacy:** select "Data Not Collected". The app has no analytics, no account and no network
requests of its own.

**Notes for the reviewer:**

```
No account is needed. Open a book from the contents, then a chapter. Tap a blue reference to see the passage in a sheet. Tap a verse number for the verse actions. All the text is in the app. Links to other apps and to the website open in the in-app browser.
```
