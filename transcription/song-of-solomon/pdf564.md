6:4 * | *Tirzah.* This city became the capital of the ten tribes, 1 Ki.15.33. The word signifies 'benevolent,' in which we have the true principle of the comparison—'beautiful because benevolent.' See 1 Co.13.1. *C.*——Tirzah, now called Telluzeh, is situated among the mountains of Samaria, a few miles north-east of Shechem. Its site is one of the most beautiful and commanding in Palestine.
