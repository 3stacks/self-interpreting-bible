[Luke, vii:11.]—"And it came to pass the day after, that he went into a city called Nain and many of his disciples went with him, and much people." Nain is to-day but a dilapidated and wretched village. It was here that our Savior met the funeral procession, and said to the dead young man: "Young man, I say unto thee, arise. And he that was dead sat up, and began to speak, and he delivered him to his mother." Perhaps this is the first instance on record where a funeral procession was changed into an assembly of good cheer and rejoicing, and Jesus Christ is the only one who has ever been able to redeem a funeral from its utter desolate and heartbreaking features, and to inspire those who are giving up their loved ones with hope to meet them again. -->
