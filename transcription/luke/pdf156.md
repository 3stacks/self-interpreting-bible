6:1 1 | *Second sabbath after the first.* Literally, 'on the second first sabbath;' but this is unintelligible, because unsuited to English idiom. The meaning most probably is, not 'the second sabbath after the first day of unleavened bread;' but, 'the *first* sabbath after the *second* day of unleavened bread,' which was the *second* after the *first* day of the passover, which was always a sabbath (Le.23.7); for upon that second day of the passover the wave sheaf was offered up, and from that *second* day, and not from the *first*, the fifty days till pentecost were reckoned (Le.23.11,15,16); whence all the sabbaths from the passover to pentecost took their numeral titles from 'the *second* of the passover.'—*C.*
