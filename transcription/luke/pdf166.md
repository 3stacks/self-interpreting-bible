9:4 2 | This precept furnishes a most valuable instruction to all who are engaged in the work of Christ. By confining them to one house, it gives them opportunity of studying character, an essential element in every preacher of the gospel who would 'rightly divide the word of truth.' 2. It enables them to form more intimate friendships, which are often of great value to themselves when they depart, and to their successors, whom they are thus qualified to introduce. 3. It prevents exposure to the dangers of mixed society, and in the midst of a life of frequent change, preserves in full vigour the domestic principle and habits.—*C.*
