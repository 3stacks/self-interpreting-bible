Ps. xxii.; 16. 10, &c. *C.* —The Jews classified the whole of the books of the Old Testament under three heads or divisions—*The Law*, including the Pentateuch; *The Prophets*, including Joshua, Judges, Samuel, Kings, and all the Prophets except Daniel; *The Psalms*, including the rest of the canonical books. This division was universally known in the time of our Lord. Consequently our Lord here uses the terms *Law*, *Prophets*, and *Psalms* as proper names; and as he declares that each division was prophetic, he thus testifies to its inspiration.—*P.*
