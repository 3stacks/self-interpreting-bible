21:3 And he said, Of a truth I say unto you, that{b} this poor widow hath cast in more than they all:
21:4 For all these have of their abundance cast in unto the offerings of God: but she of her penury hath cast in all the living that she had.
21:5 ¶ And{c} as some spake of the temple, how it was adorned with goodly stones and gifts, he said,
21:6 *As for* these things which ye behold, the days will come, in the which {d}there shall not be left one stone{2} upon another, that shall not be thrown down.
21:7 ¶ And they asked him, saying, Master, but{e} when shall these things be? and what sign *will there be* when these things shall come to pass?
21:8 And he said, {g}Take heed that ye be not deceived: for {h}many shall come in my name, saying, I am *Christ*; {i}and the time draweth near: go ye not therefore after them.
21:9 But {j}when ye shall hear of wars{3} and commotions, be not terrified: for these things must first come to pass; but {k}the end *is* not by and by.
21:10 Then{l} said he unto them, Nation shall rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom:
21:11 And great earthquakes shall be in divers places, and famines, and pestilences; and fearful sights and great signs shall there be from heaven.
21:12 But before all these, {n}they shall lay their hands on you, and persecute *you*, delivering *you* up to the synagogues, and into prisons, being brought before kings and rulers for my name's sake.
21:13 And {o}it shall turn to you for a testimony.{4}
21:14 Settle{p} *it* therefore in your hearts, not to meditate before what ye shall answer:
21:15 For{q} I will give you a mouth and wisdom, which all your adversaries shall not be able to gainsay nor resist.
21:16 And{r} ye shall be betrayed both by parents, and brethren, and kinsfolks, and friends; and *some* of you shall they cause to be put to death.
21:17 And{s} ye shall be hated of all *men* for my name's sake.
21:18 But{t} there shall not an hair of your head perish.
21:19 In{u} your patience possess ye your souls.{5}
21:20 And{v} when ye shall see Jerusalem compassed with armies, then know that the desolation thereof is nigh.{6}
21:21 Then{x} let them which are in Judea flee to the mountains; and let them which are in the midst of it depart out; and let not them that are in the countries enter thereinto.
21:22 For{y} these be the days of vengeance, that all things which are written may be fulfilled.
21:23 But{z} woe unto them that are with child, and to them that give suck, in those days! {a}for there shall be great distress in the land, and wrath upon this people.
21:24 And they shall fall by the edge of the sword, and shall be led away captive into all nations: and Jerusalem shall be trodden down of the Gentiles, {7}until the times of the Gentiles be fulfilled.{8}
21:25 ¶ And{b} there shall be signs{9} in the sun, and in the moon, and in the stars; and upon the earth{1} distress of nations, with perplexity; the sea and the waves roaring;{2}
21:26 Men's{c} hearts failing them for fear, and for looking after those things which are coming on the earth: for the powers of heaven shall be shaken.
21:27 And{d} then shall they see the Son of man coming in a cloud{3} with power and great glory.
21:28 And when these things begin to come to pass, then {e}look up, and lift up your heads; for your redemption draweth nigh.
21:29 And he spake to them a parable; {g}Behold the fig-tree, and all the trees;
21:30 When they now shoot forth, ye see and know of your ownselves that summer is now nigh at hand.
21:31 So likewise ye, {h}when ye see these things come to pass, know ye that the kingdom of God is nigh at hand.
21:32 Verily I say unto you, {i}This generation shall not pass away, till all be fulfilled.{4}
21:33 Heaven{j} and earth shall pass away: but my words shall not pass away.
21:34 ¶ And{k} take heed to yourselves, lest at any time your hearts be overcharged with {l}surfeiting, and drunkenness,{5} and cares of this life, and *so* that day come upon you unawares.
21:35 For{n} as a snare shall it come on all them that dwell on the face of the whole earth.
21:36 Watch{o} ye therefore, and pray always, that ye may be {p}accounted worthy to escape all these things that shall come to pass, and to stand before the Son of man.
21:37 And{q} in the day-time he was teaching in the temple; and at night he went out, {r}and abode in the mount that is called *the mount* of {6}Olives.
21:38 And{t} all the people came early in the morning to him in the temple, for to hear him.

## Chapter XXII.
Ver. 3. How *Satan entered into Judas*—whether personally, as the demons certainly did into the bodies of the possessed—that is, certainly, if credit is to be given to plain Scripture narrative; or, whether, by taking advantage of his corrupt dispositions, especially his avarice, and managing to present the appropriate temptations before him, and thereby suggesting correspondent ideas to his mind—has been matter of question. Guyse, Gill, Rosenmüller, &c., are of opinion that Satan entered merely by suggestion; but the identity of expression between this case and even that of the swine, Mar. 5. 13 seems clearly to decide in favour of an actual entering and possession as the means whereby Satan 'put it into the heart of Judas' to betray his Master, Jn. 13. 2. C.
