27:11 4 | The *Jewish law of inheritance* provides, (1) That on the demise of the father, the land descends to the sons. (2) If there be no sons, it descends to the daughters. (3) If no daughters, the brothers of the deceased become heirs. (4) If no brethren or paternal uncles, the land passes to the grand-uncles, or brothers of his father. (5) If there be no grand-uncles, then the nearest of kin succeeds. The law states nothing beyond the fifth degree, as in a nation constituted as Israel was, an heir could scarcely fail to be found within that degree.—*C.*
