5:6 ¶ And the king and his men {k}went to Jerusalem{l} unto the Jebusites, the inhabitants of the land; which spake unto David, saying, Except{m} thou take away the blind and the lame, thou shalt not come in hither: thinking, David cannot come in hither.
5:7 Nevertheless David took the strong hold of Zion: {n}the same *is* the city of David.
5:8 And David said on that day, {o}Whosoever getteth up to the gutter, and smiteth the Jebusites, and the lame and the blind, *that are* hated of David's soul, *he shall be chief and captain.* Wherefore they said, The blind and the lame shall not come into the house.{4}
5:9 So David dwelt in the fort, and called it, The city of David: and David built round about, from {p}Millo and inward.
5:10 And David {q}went on, and grew great; and the LORD God of hosts *was* {r}with him.
5:11 ¶ And {s}Hiram king of Tyre sent messengers to David, and cedar-trees, and carpenters, and masons;{5} and they built David an house.
5:12 And {t}David perceived that the LORD had established him king over Israel, and that he had exalted his kingdom {u}for his people Israel's sake.
5:13 ¶ And David {x}took *him* more concubines and wives out of Jerusalem, after he was come from Hebron:{6} and there were yet sons and daughters born to David.
5:14 And {y}these *be* the names of those that were born unto him in Jerusalem; {z}Shammuah, and Shobab, and Nathan, and Solomon,
5:15 Ibhar also, and {a}Elishua, and Nepheg, and Japhia,
5:16 And Elishama, and {b}Eliada, and Eliphalet.
5:17 ¶ But when the {c}Philistines heard that they had anointed David king over Israel, all the Philistines came up to seek David;{7} and David heard *of it*, and went down {d}to the hold.
5:18 The Philistines also came and spread themselves in the {e}valley of Rephaim.{8}
5:19 And David {f}inquired of the LORD, saying, Shall I go up to the Philistines? wilt thou deliver them into mine hand? And the LORD {g}said unto David, Go up; for I will doubtless deliver the Philistines into thine hand.{9}
5:20 And {h}David came to Baal-perazim, and David smote them there, and said, The LORD hath broken forth upon mine enemies before me, as the breach of waters. Therefore he called the name of that place Baal-perazim.{1}
5:21 And {i}there they left their images, and David and his men burnt them.{2}
5:22 ¶ And the Philistines came up {k}yet again, and spread themselves in the valley of {l}Rephaim.
5:23 And when David {m}inquired of the LORD, he said, Thou shalt not go up; *but* {n}fetch a compass behind them, and come upon them over against the mulberry-trees.
5:24 And let it be, when thou hearest the {o}sound of a going in the tops of the mulberry-trees, that then {p}thou shalt bestir thyself: for then shall the LORD go out before thee, to smite the host of the Philistines.
5:25 And {q}David did so, as the LORD had commanded him; and smote the Philistines from {r}Geba until thou come to Gazer.
