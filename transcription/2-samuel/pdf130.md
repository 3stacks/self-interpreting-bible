20:5 So Amasa went to assemble *the men of* Judah: but he {l}tarried longer than the set time which he had appointed him.{5}
20:6 And David said to {m}Abishai, Now shall Sheba the son of Bichri do us more harm than *did* Absalom: take thou thy {n}lord's servants, and pursue after him, lest he get him fenced cities, and escape us.{6}
20:7 And there went out after him Joab's men, and the {o}Cherethites, and the Pelethites, and all the mighty men: and they went out of Jerusalem, to pursue after Sheba the son of Bichri.
20:8 When they *were* at {p}the great stone which *is* in Gibeon, Amasa went before them: and Joab's garment that he had put on was girded unto him, and upon it a girdle *with* a sword fastened upon his loins in the sheath thereof; and as he went forth it fell out.{7}
20:9 And Joab said to Amasa, {q}*Art* thou in health, my brother? And Joab took Amasa by the beard with the right hand to kiss him.{8}
20:10 But Amasa took no heed to the sword that *was* in Joab's hand: so he {r}smote him therewith in the fifth *rib*, and shed out his bowels to the ground, and {s}struck him not again, and he died. So Joab and Abishai his brother pursued after Sheba the son of Bichri.
20:11 And one of Joab's men stood by him, and said, {t}He that favoureth Joab, and he that *is* for David, *let him go* after Joab.{9}
20:12 And Amasa {u}wallowed in blood in the midst of the highway: and when the man saw that all the people stood still, he removed Amasa out of the highway into the field, and cast a cloth upon him, when he saw that every one that came by him stood still.
20:13 When he was removed out of the highway, all the people went on after Joab, to pursue after Sheba the son of Bichri.
20:14 ¶ And {x}he went through all the tribes of Israel unto {y}Abel, and to Beth-maachah,{1} and all the Berites: and they were gathered together, and went also after him.
20:15 And they came and besieged him in Abel of Beth-maachah, and they {z}cast up a bank against the city, and {1}it stood in the {2}trench: and all the people that *were* with Joab battered the wall, to throw it down.{3}
20:16 ¶ Then cried a {a}wise woman out of the city, Hear, hear; say, I pray you, unto Joab, Come near hither, that I may speak with thee.
20:17 And when he was come near unto her, the woman said, *Art* thou Joab? And he answered, I *am he*. Then she said unto him, Hear the words of thine handmaid. And he answered, I do hear.
20:18 Then she spake, saying, {b}They were wont to speak in old time, saying, They shall surely ask *counsel* at Abel:{4} and so they ended *the matter*.
20:19 I {c}*am one of them that are* peaceable *and* faithful in Israel:{5} thou seekest to destroy a city and {d}a mother in Israel: why wilt thou {e}swallow up the inheritance of the LORD?
20:20 And Joab answered and said, Far be it, far be it from me, that I should swallow up or destroy.
20:21 The matter *is* not so: but {f}a man of mount Ephraim, (Sheba the son of Bichri by name,) hath lifted up his hand against the king, *even* against David: deliver him only, and I will depart from the city. And the woman said unto Joab, {g}Behold, his head shall be thrown to thee over the wall.
20:22 Then the woman went unto all the people {h}in her wisdom: and they cut off the head of Sheba the son of Bichri, and cast *it* out to Joab: and he {i}blew a trumpet, and they retired from the city, every man to his tent. And Joab returned to Jerusalem unto the king.
20:23 ¶ Now {k}Joab *was* over all the host of Israel: and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada *was* over the Cherethites, and over the Pelethites:{6}
20:24 And {l}Adoram *was* over the tribute: and Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud *was* {7}recorder:{8}
20:25 And Sheva *was* scribe:{9} and {m}Zadok and Abiathar *were* the priests:
20:26 And {n}Ira also the Jairite was a chief ruler{1} about David.
