22:8 Then{h} the earth shook and trembled; the foundations of heaven moved and shook, because he was wroth.

22:12 And he made {m}darkness pavilions round about him, dark{6} waters, *and* thick clouds of the skies.
22:13 Through the brightness before him were coals of fire kindled.
22:14 The {n}LORD thundered from heaven, and the most High uttered his voice.
22:15 And he {o}sent out arrows, and scattered them; lightning, and discomfited them.
22:16 And {p}the channels of the sea appeared, the foundations of the world were discovered,{7} at the rebuking of the LORD, at the blast of the breath of his nostrils.
22:17 He{q} sent from above, he took me; he drew me out of {r}many waters:
22:18 He{s} delivered me from my strong enemy, *and* from them that hated me; for they were too strong for me.
22:19 They{t} prevented me in the day of my calamity: but the LORD was my stay.
22:20 He {u}brought me forth also into a large place:{8} he delivered me, because he delighted in me.
22:21 The {x}LORD rewarded me according to my righteousness:{9} according to the cleanness of my hands hath he recompensed me.
22:22 For I have {y}kept the ways of the LORD, and have not wickedly departed from my God.
22:23 For all his {z}judgments *were* before me: and *as for* his statutes, I did not depart from them.
22:24 I {a}was also upright before{1} him, and have kept myself from mine iniquity.{2}
22:25 Therefore{b} the LORD hath recompensed me according to my righteousness; according to my cleanness in his eyesight.
22:26 With{c} the merciful thou wilt show thyself merciful, *and* with the upright man thou wilt show thyself upright.
22:27 With{d} the pure thou wilt show thyself pure; and with the froward thou wilt {e}show thyself unsavoury.
22:28 And {f}the afflicted people thou wilt save: but thine eyes *are* upon the haughty, *that* thou mayest bring *them* down.
22:29 For thou *art* my {g}lamp, O LORD: and the LORD will lighten my darkness.
22:30 For by thee I have {h}run through a troop: by my God have I leaped over a wall.
22:31 *As for* God, {i}his way *is* perfect: the word of the LORD *is* tried: he *is* a buckler to all them that trust in him.
22:32 For {k}who *is* God, save the LORD? and who *is* a rock, save our God?
22:33 God{l} *is* my strength *and* power; and he maketh my way perfect.{3}
22:34 He {m}maketh my feet like hinds' *feet*;{4} and setteth me upon my high places.
22:35 He{n} teacheth my hands to war; so that a bow of steel{5} is broken by mine arms.
22:36 Thou{o} hast also given me the shield of thy salvation: and thy gentleness hath {p}made me great.
22:37 Thou{q} hast enlarged my steps under me; so that my feet{6} did not slip.{7}
22:38 I{r} have pursued mine enemies, and destroyed them; and turned not again until I had consumed them.
22:39 And {s}I have consumed them, and wounded them, that they could not arise: yea, they are fallen under my feet.
22:40 For {t}thou hast girded me with strength to battle: them that rose up against me hast thou subdued{8} under me.
22:41 Thou {u}hast also given me the necks of mine enemies, that I might destroy them that hate me.

God's judgments should therefore make us search out and bewail our own and our fathers' iniquities. The sins of parents often entail misery on their posterity. And though the poor oppressed should have no power to relieve themselves, God will plead their cause: nor till satisfaction be made them can we hope for a blessing from God. Thus they who maliciously designed the ruin of others bring themselves and families to misery! and how unsearchable are the judgments of God in ruining the families of his wrath! Severe executions for the public good are sometimes necessary. Many parents are long spared to meet with sad scenes of anguish and grief: and often survivors, by taking pains to revive the memory of lost friends, prove their own self-tormentors. Fidelity to sovereigns in moments of danger is extremely honourable. Strength of body is of no use to resist God or withstand death. But let God's people be attacked as often as they may by their enemies, they will come off more than conquerors through him who loveth them.
