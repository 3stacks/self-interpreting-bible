3:7 And Saul had a concubine, whose name *was* {l}Rizpah, the daughter of Aiah: and *Ish-bosheth* said to Abner, Wherefore hast thou gone{m} in unto my father's concubine?{3}
3:8 Then was Abner {n}very wroth for the words of Ish-bosheth, and said, *Am* I a {o}dog's head, which against Judah do show kindness this day unto the house of Saul thy father, to his brethren, and to his friends, and have not delivered thee into the hand of David, that thou chargest me to-day with a fault concerning this woman?
3:9 So{p} do God to Abner, and more also, except, as the LORD hath {q}sworn to David, even so I do to him;
3:10 To translate the kingdom from the house of Saul, and to set up the throne of David over Israel and over Judah, {r}from Dan even to Beer-sheba.
3:11 And he could not answer Abner a word again, because he feared him.
3:12 ¶ And Abner sent messengers to David on his behalf, saying, {s}Whose *is* the land?{4} saying *also*, {t}Make thy league with me, and, behold, my hand *shall be* with thee, to bring about all Israel unto thee.
3:13 ¶ And he said, Well; I will make a league with thee: but one thing I require of thee, that is,{5} Thou{u} shalt not see my face, except thou first bring Michal,{6} Saul's daughter, when thou comest to see my face.
3:14 And David sent messengers to Ish-bosheth, Saul's son, saying, Deliver *me* {x}my wife Michal, which I espoused to me for an hundred foreskins of the Philistines.
3:15 And Ish-bosheth sent, and took her from *her* husband, *even* {y}from Phaltiel the son of Laish.
3:16 And her husband went with her along weeping{z} behind her to {a}Bahurim.{7} Then said Abner unto him, Go, return. And he returned.
3:17 ¶ And Abner had communication with the elders of Israel, saying, Ye sought for David in times past{8} *to be* king over you:
3:18 Now then do *it*; for the {b}LORD hath spoken of David, saying, {c}By the hand of my servant David I will save my people Israel out of the hand of the Philistines, and out of the hand of all their enemies.{9}
3:19 And Abner also spake in the ears of {d}Benjamin:{1} and Abner went also to speak in the ears of David in Hebron all that seemed good to Israel, and that seemed good to the whole house of Benjamin.{2}
3:20 So Abner came to David to Hebron, and twenty men with him. And {e}David made Abner, and the men that *were* with him, a feast.
3:21 And Abner said unto David, I will arise and go, and will gather {f}all Israel unto my lord the king, that they may make a league with thee, and that thou mayest reign over all that thine heart desireth. And David sent Abner away; and he went in peace.
3:22 ¶ And, behold, the servants of David and Joab came from *pursuing* a troop,{3} and brought in a great spoil with them: but Abner *was* not with David in Hebron; for he had sent him away, and he was gone in peace.
3:23 When Joab and all the host that *was* with him were come, they told Joab, saying, Abner the son of Ner came to the king, and he hath sent him away, and he is gone in peace.
3:24 Then Joab came to the king, and said, What hast thou done?{4} behold, Abner came unto thee; why *is* it *that* thou hast sent him away, and he is quite gone?{5}
3:25 Thou knowest Abner the son of Ner, that he came to {g}deceive thee, and to know thy {h}going out, and thy coming in, and to know all that thou doest.
3:26 And when Joab was come out from David, he sent messengers after Abner, which brought him again from the well of Sirah: but David knew *it* not.
3:27 And when Abner was returned to Hebron, {i}Joab took him aside in the gate to speak with him quietly,{6} and smote him there under the fifth *rib*, that he died, for the blood of Asahel his brother.
3:28 ¶ And afterward, when David heard *it*, he said, I and my kingdom *are* {k}guiltless before the LORD for ever from the {l}blood of Abner the son of Ner:
3:29 Let{m} it rest on the head of Joab, and on all his father's house; and let there not fail from the house of Joab one that hath an issue, or that is a leper, or that leaneth on a staff, or that falleth on the sword, or that lacketh bread.{7}
3:30 So Joab and Abishai his brother {n}slew Abner, because he had slain their brother Asahel at Gibeon in the battle.
3:31 ¶ And David said to Joab, and to all the people that *were* with him, {o}Rend your clothes, and gird you with sackcloth, and mourn before Abner. And king David *himself* followed the {p}bier.
3:32 And they buried Abner in Hebron: and the king lifted up his voice, and {q}wept at the grave of Abner; and all the people wept.
3:33 And the king lamented over Abner, and said, Died Abner as a fool dieth?{8}
3:34 Thy hands *were* not bound, nor thy feet put into fetters:{9} as a man falleth before wicked men,{1} *so* fellest thou.{2} And all the people {r}wept again over him.
