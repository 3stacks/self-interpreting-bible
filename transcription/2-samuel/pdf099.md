Jaffa is one of the ancient cities of the Philistines, and the woman and little girl we see on the donkey may be descendants, for all that we know, of this ancient people. We know at least that the woman is a Mohammedan by the fact that her head is covered. There is but one item in this scene that serves to distinguish it from like scenes, such as might have been witnessed in Jaffa from the days of the ancient Philistines down to the present time.
