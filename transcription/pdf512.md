## Psalm XCVI.

This Psalm was part of the hymn sung at the translation of the ark from the house of Obed-edom to the city of David, 1 Ch. xvi. 1, 23-33; but relates to the calling of the Gentiles to our Redeemer. Here are (1) Solemn calls to ascribe praises and glory to our high and great Immanuel, ver. 1-9. (2) Loud proclamations of Jesus' establishment of his gospel church, and of his coming to judge the world, to the great joy of his friends, ver. 10-13.

¶ While these high praises of God are in my mouth, let my heart behold the glory, believe the promises, and burn with desire after the enjoyment of Christ, and of God in him.

O <span class="sc">Sing</span> a new song to the Lord:
&emsp;sing all the earth to God.
2 To God sing, bless his name, show still
&emsp;his saving health abroad.
3 Among the heathen nations
&emsp;his glory do declare;
And unto all the people show
&emsp;his works that wondrous are.

4 For great's the Lord, and greatly he
&emsp;is to be magnify'd;
Yea, worthy to be fear'd is he
&emsp;above all gods beside.
5 For all the gods are idols dumb,
&emsp;which blinded nations fear;
But our God is the Lord, by whom
&emsp;the heav'ns created were.

6 Great honour is before his face,
&emsp;and majesty divine;
Strength is within his holy place,
&emsp;and there doth beauty shine.
7 Do ye ascribe unto the Lord,
&emsp;of people ev'ry tribe,
Glory do ye unto the Lord,
&emsp;and mighty pow'r ascribe.

8 Give ye the glory to the Lord
&emsp;that to his name is due;
Come ye into his courts, and bring
&emsp;an offering with you.
9 In beauty of his holiness,
&emsp;O do the Lord adore;
Likewise let all the earth throughout
&emsp;tremble his face before.

10 Among the heathen say, God reigns;
&emsp;the world shall stedfastly
Be fix'd from moving; he shall judge
&emsp;the people righteously.
11 Let heav'ns be glad before the Lord,
&emsp;and let the earth rejoice;
Let seas, and all that is therein,
&emsp;cry out, and make a noise.

12 Let fields rejoice, and ev'ry thing
&emsp;that springeth of the earth:
Then woods and ev'ry tree shall sing
&emsp;with gladness and with mirth
13 Before the Lord; because he comes,
&emsp;to judge the earth comes he:
He'll judge the world with righteousness,
&emsp;the people faithfully.

## Psalm XCVII.

This Psalm is in the same strain with the preceding; and contains (1) Solemn calls to the Gentile nations to rejoice at the equity and extent of Jesus' New Testament kingdom, and at the destruction of hardened Jews and heathen idolaters, occasioned by the establishment thereof, ver. 1-7. (2) Representations of the pure and holy joy of the saints on account of Jesus' highness, the glory of God manifested therein, and of the care taken for their safety and comfort, ver. 8-12.

¶ Join the choir, O my soul. Sing praises to thy King; sing praises with understanding.

G<span class="sc">OD</span> reigneth, let the earth be glad,
&emsp;and isles rejoice each one.
2 Dark clouds him compass; and in right
&emsp;with judgment dwells his throne.
3 Fire goes before him, and his foes
&emsp;it burns up round about:
4 His lightnings lighten did the world;
&emsp;earth saw, and shook throughout.

5 Hills, at the presence of the Lord,
&emsp;like wax, did melt away;
Ev'n at the presence of the Lord
&emsp;of all the earth, I say.
6 The heav'ns declare his righteousness,
&emsp;all men his glory see.
7 All who serve graven images,
&emsp;confounded let them be.

Who do of idols boast themselves,
&emsp;let shame upon them fall:
Ye that are called gods, see that
&emsp;ye do him worship all.
8 Sion did hear, and joyful was,
&emsp;glad Judah's daughters were;
They much rejoic'd, O Lord, because
&emsp;thy judgments did appear.

9 For thou, O Lord, art high above
&emsp;all things on earth that are;
Above all other gods thou art
&emsp;exalted very far.
10 Hate ill, all ye that love the Lord:
&emsp;his saints' souls keepeth he;
And from the hands of wicked men
&emsp;he sets them safe and free.

11 For all those that be righteous
&emsp;sown is a joyful light,
And gladness sown is for all those
&emsp;that are in heart upright.
12 Ye righteous, in the Lord rejoice;
&emsp;express your thankfulness,
When ye into your memory
&emsp;do call his holiness.

## Psalm XCVIII.

This Psalm insists on the same subject as the two preceding; and contains (1) Demands of praise to the Redeemer, for the wonders he hath wrought, the victories he hath won, the discoveries he hath made, and the promises he fulfils, ver. 1-3. (2) Demands of universal and high rejoicing at the erection and establishment of his gospel church, and at his coming in the clouds to judge the world, ver. 4-9.

¶ While my lips utter these lofty notes, let my soul, according to this time also, say, What hath the Lord wrought! what is Jesus, what is <span class="sc">Jehovah</span> to my heart! what hath he done for mankind, and what for my soul! what of Jesus, what of <span class="sc">Jehovah</span> have mine eyes seen and my heart tasted!—and while I assuredly believe his return to judgment, let me with ardour look for that blessed hope, and glorious appearing of the great <span class="sc">God</span> my Saviour. Let my soul with rapture spring forth to meet his burning chariot-wheels.

O <span class="sc">Sing</span> a new song to the Lord,
&emsp;for wonders he hath done:
His right hand and his holy arm
&emsp;him victory hath won.
2 The Lord God his salvation
&emsp;hath caused to be known;
His justice in the heathen's sight
&emsp;he openly hath shown.

3 He mindful of his grace and truth
&emsp;to Isr'el's house hath been;
And the salvation of our God
&emsp;all ends of th' earth have seen.
4 Let all the earth unto the Lord
&emsp;send forth a joyful noise;
Lift up your voice aloud to him,
&emsp;sing praises, and rejoice.

5 With harp, with harp, and voice of psalms,
&emsp;unto <span class="sc">Jehovah</span> sing:
6 With trumpets, cornets, gladly sound
&emsp;before the Lord the King.
7 Let seas and all their fulness roar;
&emsp;the world, and dwellers there;
8 Let floods clap hands, and let the hills
&emsp;together joy declare

9 Before the Lord; because he comes,
&emsp;to judge the earth comes he;
He'll judge the world with righteousness,
&emsp;his folk with equity.

## Psalm XCIX.

This Psalm is of the same general nature with the three preceding; and calls men to revere, worship and praise the Messiah and his Father (1) For the glory, greatness, mercy, and equity of his government, ver. 1-5. (2) For the instances of his kindness to Israel, in remarkably regarding and honouring their noted deliverers and reformers, ver. 6-9.

¶ While I sing this, let my soul stand in awe of <span class="sc">Jehovah</span>'s greatness and excellency. Let me call on God in every time of need, that he may answer and save me. Let me hope that he will forgive and subdue, but never will indulge, my transgressions; for the Lord my God is holy.

T<span class="sc">H'</span> eternal Lord doth reign as king,
&emsp;let all the people quake;
He sits between the cherubim,
&emsp;let th' earth be mov'd and shake.
2 The Lord in Sion great and high
&emsp;above all people is;
3 Thy great and dreadful name (for it
&emsp;is holy) let them bless.

4 The king's strength also judgment loves;
&emsp;thou settlest equity:
Just judgment thou dost execute
&emsp;in Jacob righteously.
5 The Lord our God exalt on high,
&emsp;and rev'rently do ye
Before his footstool worship him:
&emsp;the Holy One is he.

6 Moses and Aaron 'mong his priests,
&emsp;Samuel, with them that call
Upon his name: these call'd on God,
&emsp;and he them answer'd all.
7 Within the pillar of the cloud
&emsp;he unto them did speak:
The testimonies he them taught,
&emsp;and laws, they did not break.

8 Thou answer'dst them, O Lord our God;
&emsp;thou wast a God that gave
Pardon to them, though on their deeds
&emsp;thou wouldest vengeance have.
9 Do ye exalt the Lord our God,
&emsp;and at his holy hill
Do ye him worship: for the Lord
&emsp;our God is holy still.

## Psalm C.

*A Psalm of praise.*

This Psalm (1) Contains a solemn call to the Gentiles to praise God and rejoice in him, ver. 1-4. (2) Furnishes us with matters of praise, viz. that he is God, and stands nearly related to us; and that his mercy and truth are everlasting, ver. 3-5.
