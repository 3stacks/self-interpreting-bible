61 Bands of ill men me robb'd; yet I
&emsp;thy precepts did not slight.

62 I'll rise at midnight thee to praise,
&emsp;ev'n for thy judgments right.

63 I am companion to all those
&emsp;who fear, and thee obey.

64 O Lord, thy mercy fills the earth:
&emsp;teach me thy laws, I pray.

TETH. *Part 9th.*

Contains (1) Hearty acknowledgments of God's faithfulness and goodness, and of the excellency and usefulness of his word, ver. 65, 68, 72. (2) Humble acknowledgments of the advantage he had received from his afflictions, ver. 67, 71. (3) Complaints of the pride, calumnious reproach, and luxurious stupidity, of wicked men, ver. 69, 70. (4) Supplications for divine instruction, ver. 66, 68. (5) Declarations of his regard to God's word, and resolutions to persevere in the observance thereof, ver. 66-72.

65 Well hast thou with thy servant dealt,
&emsp;as thou didst promise give.

66 Good judgment me, and knowledge teach,
&emsp;for I thy word believe.

67 Ere I afflicted was I stray'd;
&emsp;but now I keep thy word.

68 Both good thou art, and good thou do'st:
&emsp;teach me thy statutes, Lord.

69 The men that are puff'd up with pride
&emsp;against me forg'd a lie;
Yet thy commandements observe
&emsp;with my whole heart will I.

70 Their hearts, through worldly ease and wealth,
&emsp;as fat as grease they be:
But in thy holy law I take
&emsp;delight continually.

71 It hath been very good for me
&emsp;that I afflicted was,
That I might well instructed be,
&emsp;and learn thy holy laws.

72 The word that cometh from thy mouth
&emsp;is better unto me
Than many thousands and great sums
&emsp;of gold and silver be.

JOD. *Part 10th.*

Contains (1) Thankful acknowledgments of God's kindness in creation, and of his equity and faithfulness in afflicting, ver. 73, 75. (2) Earnest supplications for divine instruction; for fresh discoveries of God's mercy; for the confusion of enemies, the comfortable fellowship of friends, and for an honourable soundness of heart, ver. 73, 76, 77, 79, 80. (3) Holy joy in the comfort of fellow saints, and especially in God's mercy and word, ver. 74, 76, 77. (4) A holy resolution to meditate on, and cleave to, God's testimonies amidst persecution. ver. 78.

73 Thou mad'st and fashion'dst me: thy laws
&emsp;to know give wisdom, Lord.

74 So who thee fear shall joy to see
&emsp;me trusting in thy word.

75 That very right thy judgments are
&emsp;I know, and do confess;
And that thou hast afflicted me
&emsp;in truth and faithfulness.

76 O let thy kindness merciful,
&emsp;I pray thee, comfort me,
As to thy servant faithfully
&emsp;was promised by thee.

77 And let thy tender mercies come
&emsp;to me, that I may live;
Because thy holy laws to me
&emsp;sweet delectation give.

78 Lord, let the proud ashamed be;
&emsp;for they, without a cause,
With me perversely dealt: but I
&emsp;will muse upon thy laws.

79 Let such as fear thee, and have known
&emsp;thy statutes, turn to me.

80 My heart let in thy laws be sound,
&emsp;that sham'd I never be.

CAPH. *Part 11th.*

Contains (1) Bitter complaints of great and long distress, ver. 81-87. (2) Fervent supplications for speedy comfort, help, and quickening, ver. 82, 86, 88. (3) Pleas wherewith these supplications are enforced; as that his distress was become insupportable; that his hope was in God's word, and he clave to it in his profession and practice; that his life was but short to have so much of it spent in the furnace of affliction; that the malice and pride of his enemies were excessive; and, in fine, that God's loving-kindness was infinite, ver. 81-88.

81 My soul for thy salvation faints;
&emsp;yet I thy word believe.

82 Mine eyes fail for thy word: I say,
&emsp;When wilt thou comfort give?

83 For like a bottle I'm become,
&emsp;that in the smoke is set:
I'm black, and parch'd with grief; yet I
&emsp;thy statutes not forget.

84 How many are thy servant's days?
&emsp;when wilt thou execute
Just judgment on these wicked men
&emsp;that do me persecute?

85 The proud have digged pits for me,
&emsp;which is against thy laws.

86 Thy words all faithful are: help me,
&emsp;pursu'd without a cause.

87 They so consum'd me, that on earth
&emsp;my life they scarce did leave:
Thy precepts yet forsook I not,
&emsp;but close to them did cleave.

88 After thy loving-kindness, Lord,
&emsp;me quicken, and preserve:
The testimony of thy mouth
&emsp;so shall I still observe.

LAMED. *Part 12th.*

Represents (1) The eternity and unchangeableness of God's word and works, ver. 89-91. (2) The advantage of delighting in, and cleaving to, God's truths, ver. 92, 93. (3) God's interest in men a reason of his saving the observers of his law, ver. 94. (4) David's adherence to God's truths amidst furious persecution, ver. 95. (5) The superlative excellency of God's word above all earthly enjoyments, ver. 96.

89 Thy word for ever is, O Lord,
&emsp;in heaven settled fast;

90 Unto all generations
&emsp;thy faithfulness doth last:
The earth thou hast established,
&emsp;and it abides by thee.

91 This day they stand as thou ordain'dst,
&emsp;for all thy servants be.

92 Unless in thy most perfect law
&emsp;my soul delights had found,
I should have perished, when as
&emsp;my troubles did abound.

93 Thy precepts I will ne'er forget;
&emsp;they quick'ning to me brought.

94 Lord, I am thine; O save thou me:
&emsp;thy precepts I have sought.

95 For me the wicked have laid wait,
&emsp;me seeking to destroy:
But I thy testimonies true
&emsp;consider will with joy.

96 An end of all perfection
&emsp;here have I seen, O God:
But as for thy commandement,
&emsp;it is exceeding broad.

MEM. *Part 13th.*

Represents the Psalmist's ardent affection to God's law, ver. 97. Because (1) Thereby he had attained much useful knowledge and wisdom, ver. 98-100; (2) Thereby he was enabled to refrain from, and hate sin, ver. 101, 104; (3) Thereby he was preserved steady in the path of duty, ver. 101, 102; and (4) Thereby he obtained much comfort to his soul, ver. 103.

97 O how love I thy law! it is
&emsp;my study all the day:

98 It makes me wiser than my foes;
&emsp;for it doth with me stay.

99 Than all my teachers now I have
&emsp;more understanding far;
Because my meditation
&emsp;thy testimonies are.

100 In understanding I excel
&emsp;those that are ancients;
For I endeavoured to keep
&emsp;all thy commandements.

101 My feet from each ill way I stay'd,
&emsp;that I may keep thy word.

102 I from thy judgments have not swerv'd;
&emsp;for thou hast taught me, Lord.

103 How sweet unto my taste, O Lord,
&emsp;are all thy words of truth!
Yea, I do find them sweeter far
&emsp;than honey to my mouth.

104 I through thy precepts, that are pure,
&emsp;do understanding get;
I therefore ev'ry way that's false
&emsp;with all my heart do hate.

NUN. *Part 14th.*

Represents (1) The remarkable usefulness of God's word, ver. 105. (2) David's solemn dedication of himself to God's service, ver. 106. (3) His recourse to God by prayer under his afflictions, ver. 107, 108. (4) His faithful adherence to God's way amidst trouble and persecution, ver. 109, 110. (5) his solemn and deliberate choice of God's word for his portion and rule, ver. 111, 112.

105 Thy word is to my feet a lamp,
&emsp;and to my path a light.

106 I sworn have, and I will perform,
&emsp;to keep thy judgments right.

107 I am with sore affliction
&emsp;ev'n overwhelm'd, O Lord:
In mercy raise and quicken me,
&emsp;according to thy word.

108 The free-will-off'rings of my mouth
&emsp;accept, I thee beseech:
And unto me thy servant, Lord,
&emsp;thy judgments clearly teach.

109 Though still my soul be in my hand,
&emsp;thy laws I'll not forget.

110 I err'd not from them, though for me
&emsp;the wicked snares did set.

111 I of thy testimonies have
&emsp;above all things made choice,
To be my heritage for aye;
&emsp;for they my heart rejoice.

112 I carefully inclined have
&emsp;my heart still to attend;
That I thy statutes may perform
&emsp;alway unto the end.

SAMECH. *Part 15th.*

We have here (1) David's hatred of sin and love of God's law, ver. 113. (2) His profession of dependence upon God, ver. 114. (3) His debarring of wicked men from his company, ver. 115. (4) His prayer for support, comfort, and honour, in the Lord's way, 116, 117. (5) His foresight of, and trembling at, the ruin of the wicked, ver. 118-120.

113 I hate the thoughts of vanity,
&emsp;but love thy law do I.

114 My shield and hiding-place thou art:
&emsp;I on thy word rely.

115 All ye that evil-doers are
&emsp;from me depart away;
For the commandments of my God
&emsp;I purpose to obey.

116 According to thy faithful word
&emsp;uphold and stablish me,
That I may live, and of my hope
&emsp;ashamed never be.

117 Hold thou me up, so shall I be
&emsp;in peace and safety still;
And to thy statutes have respect
&emsp;continually I will.

118 Thou tread'st down all that love to stray;
&emsp;false their deceit doth prove.

119 Lewd men, like dross, away thou putt'st;
&emsp;therefore thy law I love.

120 For fear of thee my very flesh
&emsp;doth tremble, all dismay'd;
And of thy righteous judgments, Lord,
&emsp;my soul is much afraid.

AIN. *Part 16th.*

Here David (1) Appeals to God as to his integrity and equity, ver. 121. (2) Supplicates that God would protect him against enemies; grant him merciful deliverance and saving instruction, and arise for the maintenance of his own honour, ver. 121-126.
