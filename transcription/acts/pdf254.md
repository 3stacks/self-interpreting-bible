<!-- summary -->
1 Peter preaching to the people that came to see a lame man restored to his feet, 12 professeth the cure not to have been wrought by his or John's own power, or holiness, but by God, and his Son Jesus, and through faith in his name: 13 withal reprehending them for crucifying Jesus. 17 Which, because they did it through ignorance, and that thereby were fulfilled God's determinate counsel, and the scriptures: 19 he exhorteth them by repentance and faith to seek remission of their sins, and salvation in the same Jesus.

3:1 Now {a}Peter and John went up together into the temple at the hour of prayer, being the ninth hour.{1}
3:2 And a certain man {b}lame from his mother's womb was carried, whom they laid daily at the gate of the temple which is called Beautiful,{2} to ask{c} alms of them that entered into the temple;
3:3 Who, seeing Peter and John about to go into the temple, asked an alms.
3:4 And {d}Peter, fastening his eyes upon him with John, said, Look on us.
3:5 And he gave heed unto them, expecting to receive something of them.
3:6 Then Peter said, {e}Silver and gold have I none; but such as I have give I thee: In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, rise up and walk.{3}
3:7 And he took him by the right hand, and lifted him up: and {g}immediately his feet and ankle bones received strength.
3:8 And {h}he, leaping up, stood, and walked, and entered with them into the temple, walking, and leaping, and praising God.
3:9 And{i} all the people saw him walking and praising God:
3:10 And they knew that it was he which sat for alms at the Beautiful gate of the temple: and {j}they were filled with wonder and amazement at that which had happened unto him.
3:11 And, as the lame man which was healed held Peter and John, all the people ran together unto them, in {l}the porch that is called Solomon's, greatly wondering.
3:12 ¶ And when Peter saw *it*, he answered unto the people, Ye men of Israel, why marvel ye at this? or why look ye so earnestly on us, as{n} though by our own power or holiness we had made this man to walk?
3:13 The{o} God of Abraham, and of Isaac, and of Jacob, the God of our fathers, hath {p}glorified his Son Jesus; whom {q}ye delivered up, and denied him in the presence of Pilate,{4} when he was determined to let *him* go.
3:14 But{r} ye denied the Holy One and the Just, and desired a murderer to be granted unto you;
3:15 And killed the Prince{5} of life, {s}whom God hath raised from the dead; whereof we are witnesses.
3:16 And his name,{6} through {t}faith in his name, hath made this man strong, whom ye see and know: yea, the faith which is by him hath given him this perfect soundness in the presence of you all.
3:17 And now, brethren, I wot that through ignorance{7} ye did *it*, as did also your rulers.
3:18 But{u} those things, which God before had showed by the mouth of all his prophets, that Christ should suffer, he hath so fulfilled.
3:19 Repent{v} ye therefore, and be {x}converted, that your sins may be blotted out, when {y}the times of refreshing shall come from the presence of the Lord;
3:20 And he shall {z}send Jesus Christ, which {a}before was preached unto you:
3:21 Whom{b} the heaven must receive until the times of {c}restitution of all things, which God hath spoken by the mouth of all his holy prophets since the world began.
3:22 For Moses truly said unto the fathers, {d}A prophet shall the Lord your God {e}raise up unto you of your brethren, {g}like unto me; him shall {h}ye hear in all things whatsoever he shall say unto you.
3:23 And it shall come to pass, {i}that every soul, which will not hear that Prophet, shall be destroyed from among the people.
3:24 Yea{j} and all the prophets from Samuel, and those that follow after, as many as have spoken, have likewise foretold of these days.{8}
3:25 Ye{k} are the children of the prophets, and of the covenant which God made with our fathers, saying unto Abraham, {l}And in thy seed shall all the kindreds of the earth be blessed.
3:26 Unto{n} you first God, having raised up his Son Jesus, sent him to {o}bless you, in turning away every one of you from his iniquities.{9}

How open their avowal of Jesus' messiahship, divinity, and resurrection from the dead, where he had been lately crucified as a notorious malefactor! Great is the wisdom and power of God in thus overruling the wickedness of men for his own glory, for the honour of Christ, and the salvation of men. But his purpose, or overruling providence, in no respect excuses or extenuates our guilt. Alas! how perversely men will continue opposing and reviling the most evident manifestations of divine power and goodness till the Holy Ghost begins his work in their heart! But then the most wonderful effects will follow—the deepest impressions of divine things; a thorough conviction and self-debasement; earnest inquiries after salvation; the most unfeigned faith and sincere repentance, and their children to the Lord Jesus Christ and to his gospel; fellowship with his people; delightful ordinances; grateful praise for his kindness, and zeal for his glory; heavenly-mindedness, contempt of this world, and compassionate love to poor fellow-saints; and a daily addition of members to the church.

## Chapter III.

Ver. 19. *Repent.* Change your minds.—*Be converted.* Change your conduct—doing both, not in order to believing, but as the necessary consequences of believing the gospel. *The times of restitution.* The times when all promises have been accomplished in the restoration of the Jews and the conversion of the Gentiles;—when Christ shall be acknowledged as King in Zion, and head over all things to the church.—Even so, come, Lord Jesus—come quickly! *C.*
