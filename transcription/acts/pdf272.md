10:22+ was {z}warned from God by an holy angel, to send for thee into his house, and to hear words of thee.
10:23 Then called he them in, and lodged *them*. And{a} on the morrow Peter went away with them, and certain brethren from Joppa accompanied him.
10:24 And the morrow after they entered into Cesarea. And Cornelius waited for them, and had called together his kinsmen and near friends.
10:25 ¶ And as Peter was coming in, Cornelius met him, and fell down at his feet, and {b}worshipped *him*.{4}
10:26 But Peter took him up, saying, {c}Stand up; I myself also am a man.
10:27 And as he talked with him, he went in, and found {d}many that were come together.
10:28 And he said unto them, Ye know {e}how that it is an unlawful thing for a man that is a Jew to keep company, or come unto one of another nation; but God hath showed me {g}that I should not call any man common or unclean.
10:29 Therefore came I unto you without gainsaying, {h}as soon as I was sent for: I ask therefore for what intent ye have sent for me?
10:30 And Cornelius said, Four days ago I was fasting{i} until this hour: and at the ninth hour I prayed in my house, and, behold, a man stood before me in {j}bright clothing,
10:31 And{k} said, Cornelius, thy prayer is heard, and thine alms are had in remembrance in the sight of God.{5}
10:32 Send therefore to Joppa, and call hither Simon, whose surname is Peter; he is lodged in the house of *one* Simon a tanner, by the sea-side: who, when he cometh, shall speak unto thee.
10:33 Immediately therefore I sent to thee; and thou hast well done that thou art come. Now therefore are we all here present before God, {l}to hear all things that are commanded thee of God.
10:34 ¶ Then Peter {n}opened *his* mouth, and said, {o}Of a truth I perceive that God is no respecter of persons:
10:35 But{p} in every nation he that feareth him,{6} and worketh righteousness, is accepted with him.
10:36 The{q} word which God sent unto the children of Israel, preaching peace by Jesus Christ: (he {r}is Lord of all:)
10:37 That word, *I* say, ye know, {s}which was published throughout all Judea, and began from Galilee, after the baptism which John preached;
10:38 How {t}God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Ghost and with power: who {u}went about doing good, and healing all that were oppressed of the devil; for {v}God was with him.
10:39 And {x}we are witnesses of all things which he did, both in the land of the Jews, and in Jerusalem; whom {y}they slew and hanged on a tree:
10:40 Him God raised up the third day, and showed him openly;
10:41 {z}Not to all the people, but unto witnesses chosen before of God, even to us, who did eat and drink with him after he rose from the dead.
10:42 And{a} he commanded us to preach unto the people, and to testify that it is he {b}which was ordained of God *to be* the Judge of quick and dead.
10:43 {c}To him give all the prophets witness, that through his name whosoever believeth in him shall receive remission of sins.{7}
10:44 ¶ While{d} Peter yet spake these words, the Holy Ghost fell on all them which heard the word.
10:45 And they of the circumcision {e}which believed were astonished, as many as came with Peter, because that on the Gentiles also was poured out the gift of the Holy Ghost.
10:46 For they heard them {g}speak with tongues, and magnify God. Then answered Peter,
10:47 {h}Can any man forbid water, that these should not be baptized, which have received the Holy Ghost as well as we?
10:48 And he commanded them {i}to be baptized in the name of the Lord. {j}Then prayed they him to tarry certain days.

10:35 6 | *He that feareth him* (God) is he that begins with 'wisdom' (Ps. 111.10)—that is, who begins with taking Jesus Christ as his 'wisdom' (1 Co. 1.24) as his only and all-sufficient guide to salvation; and he 'that worketh righteousness' is he who, having heard the law pronounce himself and all men 'guilty before God' (Ro. 3.19), has thereby discovered 'that there is none righteous, no not one' (Ro. 3.10), in whom God, by his Spirit, 'worketh both to will and to do of his good pleasure' (Phi. 2.13), which 'willing' and 'doing' terminate in receiving 'Christ Jesus, who of God is made unto us wisdom, righteousness, sanctification, and redemption,' 1 Co. 1.30.—*Note*, Those who attempt to pervert the words of Peter to the establishment of human righteousness, independent of, or additional to, 'the righteousness of God by faith of Jesus Christ,' do so by seizing upon an insulated text, and neglecting to 'compare spiritual with spiritual,' the most essential canon of all scriptural interpretation.—C.
