In the picture we have a view of Bethel as it is to-day. It is twelve miles from Jerusalem, and is a place where tourists take lunch the first day out from the Holy City. It is a desolate looking place, and the ruins of the ancient city are about three or four acres in extent. These consist of foundations, fragments of walls, and large heaps of stone. In the picture we are looking toward the northeast. South of the village there is a great reservoir. Every stone and ruin about this old place speaks of the past. Bethel means "The House of God."
