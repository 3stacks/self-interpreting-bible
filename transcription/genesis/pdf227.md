## Chapter VIII.
Ver. 1. God's *remembering* persons or things denotes his manifesting his notice of them, either in a way of mercy for their deliverance or benefit, according to his promise, as here, and ch. 19. 29; 30. 22; Ex. 32. 13; Ne. 13. 14, 22; Job 14. 13; Ps. 132. 1; 106. 4; 136. 23; or in a way of wrath for their punishment, Ps. 137. 7; Am. 8. 7; Re. 16. 19; 18. 5.

Ver. 12. [It is important to note the periods of seven days which are repeatedly mentioned in this narrative. They indicate that the creation *week* still regulated the smaller divisions of time, and that the Sabbath was observed by the family of Noah. *P.*]
