We give the view of the Spring of the Virgin because it is in the neighborhood where Joseph lived and where his father met him. The water in this region is such as has percolated through the soil from the Nile. Eber says that this spring is mentioned in records of the highest antiquity. It was believed for centuries that the balsam shrub could thrive here and nowhere else. This spot has come to be dear to the human race because the holy family sojourned here during their stay in Egypt, and because of its connection with Moses and Joseph. -->
