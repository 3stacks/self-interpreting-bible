<!-- illustration: CHANGING HORSES ON THE ROAD TO BEYROUT—THIS PHOTOGRAPH TAKEN ON TOP OF THE LEBANON MOUNTAINS. [Zechariah, xi:1.]—"Open thy doors, O Lebanon, that the fire may devour thy cedars." The artist and the writer left Damascus at 4:30 o'clock in the morning by a diligence to go to Beyrout. This is a ride of seventy miles over the Lebanon mountains, and is made in thirteen hours. Horses are changed every hour. Six are used to pull the conveyance and much of the time they pass in a sweeping gallop. These horses are being changed near the top of the Lebanon mountains, and the time is near the middle of the day. The Bible represents Lebanon as lying on the northern border of the promised land. This mountain has always been famous for cedars. Some distinguished traveler has said: "I have traveled in no part of the world where I have seen another such variety of glorious mountain scenes within so narrow a compass." -->
