In the fields and plains around this aqueduct we are told that one went to gather herbs to make pottage for the sons of the prophets, when he found a wild vine and gathered wild gourds from it to mix with the pottage. Elijah and Elisha passed this way from Jericho to Jordan. It is in the neighborhood of the city of palm trees. -->
