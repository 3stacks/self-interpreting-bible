We give this view of the Pnyx from Mars Hill to illustrate a scene in Athens over which Codrus reigned at the very time that Saul ruled over the Israelites. The Pnyx is a huge artificial terrace or platform 395 feet long by 312 feet wide, the upper margin of which is cut out of the rock.
