ALEPH. *Part 1st.*

Here (1) David marks out holy men as the only blessed men, ver. 1-3. (2) Begs that, to his own honour and satisfaction, he may be helped of God to an upright observance of his laws, ver. 4-6. (3) In the faith of God's direction and assistance, he resolves to praise and obey him, ver. 7, 8.

B<span class="sc">LESSED</span> are they that undefil'd,
&emsp;and straight are in the way;
Who in the Lord's most holy law
&emsp;do walk, and do not stray.

2 Blessed are they who to observe
&emsp;his statutes are inclin'd;
And who do seek the living God
&emsp;with their whole heart and mind.

3 Such in his ways do walk, and they
&emsp;do no iniquity.

4 Thou hast commanded us to keep
&emsp;thy precepts carefully.

5 O that thy statutes to observe
&emsp;thou would'st my ways direct!

6 Then shall I not be sham'd, when I
&emsp;thy precepts all respect.

7 Then with integrity of heart
&emsp;thee will I praise and bless,
When I the judgments all have learn'd
&emsp;of thy pure righteousness.

8 That I will keep thy statutes all
&emsp;firmly resolv'd have I:
O do not then, most gracious God,
&emsp;forsake me utterly.

BETH. *Part 2d.*

He (1) Points out the proper method of mortifying lusts, and attaining true holiness, ver. 9. (2) Professes his attachment to God and his word, and supplicates wisdom, direction, and support, ver. 10-12. (3) Reflects, with satisfaction, on his former regard to God's truths and ways, ver. 13, 14. And (4) Resolves to persevere therein, ver. 15, 16.

9 By what means shall a young man learn
&emsp;his way to purify?
If he according to thy word
&emsp;thereto attentive be.

10 Unfeignedly thee have I sought
&emsp;with all my soul and heart:
O let me not from the right path
&emsp;of thy commands depart.

11 Thy word I in my heart have hid,
&emsp;that I offend not thee.

12 O Lord, thou ever blessed art,
&emsp;thy statutes teach thou me.

13 The judgments of thy mouth each one
&emsp;my lips declared have:

14 More joy thy testimonies' way
&emsp;than riches all me gave.

15 I will thy holy precepts make
&emsp;my meditation;
And carefully I'll have respect
&emsp;unto thy ways each one.

16 Upon thy statutes my delight
&emsp;shall constantly be set:
And, by thy grace, I never will
&emsp;thy holy word forget.

GIMEL. *Part 3d.*

(1) With eagerness and longings of heart he supplicates divine favour and instruction in God's truths, ver. 17-20. (2) Remarks the just dealings of God upon such as wander from his ways, ver. 21. (3) Supplicates preservation from reproach, as he observed, meditated on, delighted in, and consulted God's word in all his conduct, ver. 22-24.

17 With me thy servant, in thy grace,
&emsp;deal bountifully, Lord;
That by thy favour I may live,
&emsp;and duly keep thy word.

18 Open mine eyes, that of thy law
&emsp;the wonders I may see.

19 I am a stranger on this earth,
&emsp;hide not thy laws from me.

20 My soul within me breaks, and doth
&emsp;much fainting still endure,
Through longing that it hath all times
&emsp;unto thy judgments pure.

21 Thou hast rebuk'd the cursed proud,
&emsp;who from thy precepts swerve.

22 Reproach and shame remove from me,
&emsp;for I thy laws observe.

23 Against me princes spake with spite,
&emsp;while they in council sat:
But I thy servant did upon
&emsp;thy statutes meditate.

24 My comfort, and my heart's delight,
&emsp;thy testimonies be;
And they, in all my doubts and fears,
&emsp;are counsellors to me.

DALETH. *Part 4th.*

He (1) Represents his distress, and his behaviour under it, ver. 25, 26, 28, 30, 31. (2) Supplicates spiritual quickening, instruction, strength, rectitude of sentiment, and honour, in the Lord's way, ver. 25-31. (3) Resolves, under the influences of heaven, to be more active in holiness, ver. 32.

25 My soul to dust cleaves: quicken me,
&emsp;according to thy word.

26 My ways I show'd, and me thou heard'st:
&emsp;teach me thy statutes, Lord.

27 The way of thy commandements
&emsp;make me aright to know;
So all thy works that wondrous are
&emsp;I shall to others show.

28 My soul doth melt, and drop away,
&emsp;for heaviness and grief:
To me, according to thy word,
&emsp;give strength, and send relief.

29 From me the wicked way of lies
&emsp;let far removed be;
And graciously thy holy law
&emsp;do thou grant unto me.

30 I chosen have the perfect way
&emsp;of truth and verity:
Thy judgments that most righteous are
&emsp;before me laid have I.

31 I to thy testimonies cleave;
&emsp;shame do not on me cast.

32 I'll run thy precepts' way, when thou
&emsp;my heart enlarged hast.

HE. *Part 5th.*

He (1) Prays for divine instructions, sanctifying influences, and quickening grace; for the accomplishment of God's promises to him;—and against covetousness, vanity, and reproach, ver. 33-40. (2) Enforces his requests from his holy resolutions, his relation to God, his zeal for his honour, and his regard to his excellent declarations, promises, and precepts, ver. 33, 34, 38-40.

33 Teach me, O Lord, the perfect way
&emsp;of thy precepts divine,
And to observe it to the end
&emsp;I shall my heart incline.

34 Give understanding unto me,
&emsp;so keep thy law shall I;
Yea, ev'n with my whole heart I shall
&emsp;observe it carefully.

35 In thy law's path make me to go;
&emsp;for I delight therein.

36 My heart unto thy testimonies,
&emsp;and not to greed, incline.

37 Turn thou away my sight and eyes
&emsp;from viewing vanity;
And in thy good and holy way
&emsp;be pleas'd to quicken me.

38 Confirm to me thy gracious word,
&emsp;which I did gladly hear,
Ev'n to thy servant, Lord, who is
&emsp;devoted to thy fear.

39 Turn thou away my fear'd reproach;
&emsp;for good thy judgments be.

40 Lo, for thy precepts I have long'd;
&emsp;in thy truth quicken me.

VAU. *Part 6th.*

Here are (1) The prayers he presented to God, ver. 41, 43. (2) The graces he exercised upon God and his word, *faith, hope, love,* ver. 42, 43, 47, 48. (3) The practice he resolved on, through grace: to keep God's law; to seek and cheerfully practise his precepts; boldly publish his truth to others; and to delight in, meditate on, and zealously reduce them to practice, ver. 44-48.

41 Let thy sweet mercies also come
&emsp;and visit me, O Lord;
Ev'n thy benign salvation,
&emsp;according to thy word.

42 So shall I have wherewith I may
&emsp;give him an answer just,
Who spitefully reproacheth me;
&emsp;for in thy word I trust.

43 The word of truth out of my mouth
&emsp;take thou not utterly;
For on thy judgments righteous
&emsp;my hope doth still rely.

44 So shall I keep for evermore
&emsp;thy law continually.

45 And, sith that I thy precepts seek,
&emsp;I'll walk at liberty.

46 I'll speak thy word to kings, and I
&emsp;with shame shall not be mov'd;

47 And will delight myself always
&emsp;in thy laws, which I lov'd.

48 To thy commandments, which I lov'd,
&emsp;my hands lift up I will;
And I will also meditate
&emsp;upon thy statutes still.

ZAIN. *Part 7th.*

Represents (1) The comfort he had found in God's word, ver. 49, 50, 52, 54. (2) His desire that God would fulfil his promises, ver. 49; and his care to remember, think on, and practise God's truths, ver. 51, 52, 55, 56. (3) The injurious reproach he suffered from the wicked; his abhorrence of their sin, and dread of their punishment, ver. 51, 53.

49 Remember, Lord, thy gracious word
&emsp;thou to thy servant spake,
Which, for a ground of my sure hope,
&emsp;thou causedst me to take.

50 This word of thine my comfort is
&emsp;in mine affliction:
For in my straits I am reviv'd
&emsp;by this thy word alone.

51 The men whose hearts with pride are stuff'd
&emsp;did greatly me deride;
Yet from thy straight commandements
&emsp;I have not turn'd aside.

52 Thy judgments righteous, O Lord,
&emsp;which thou of old forth gave,
I did remember, and myself
&emsp;by them comforted have.

53 Horror took hold on me, because
&emsp;ill men thy law forsake.

54 I in my house of pilgrimage
&emsp;thy laws my songs do make.

55 Thy name by night, Lord, I did mind,
&emsp;and I have kept thy law.

56 And this I had, because thy word
&emsp;I kept, and stood in awe.

CHETH. *Part 8th.*

We have here (1) The portion he chose, ver. 57. (2) The purpose he formed, ver. 57. (3) The prayers he offered up for favour, mercy, and instruction, ver. 58-64. (4) The penitential care he took to obey God's law, ver. 59-61. (5) The complaint he uttered, ver. 61. (6) His fervent thanksgiving, ver. 62. (7) His choice companions, ver. 63. (8) His deep sense of God's abundant mercy and grace, ver. 64.

57 Thou my sure portion art alone,
&emsp;which I did chuse, O Lord:
I have resolv'd, and said, that I
&emsp;would keep thy holy word.

58 With my whole heart I did entreat
&emsp;thy face and favour free:
According to thy gracious word
&emsp;be merciful to me.

59 I thought upon my former ways,
&emsp;and did my life well try;
And to thy testimonies pure
&emsp;my feet then turned I.

60 I did not stay, nor linger long,
&emsp;as those that slothful are;
But hastily thy laws to keep
&emsp;myself I did prepare.
