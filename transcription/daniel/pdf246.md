## Chapter II.
Ver. 31-45. This great *image*, beheld in a quickly forgotten dream, represented the successive empires in the world as but an empty show, a terrible but quickly forgotten vision. The *golden head* was the Chaldean monarchy, extremely rich, and in appearance lasting, including the Assyrian; it continued about 240 years. The *breast* and *arms of silver* signified the Medo-Persian, established by Cyrus, which lasted about 204 years.
