1 Upon the reading of the law separation is made from the mixed multitude. 4 Nehemiah at his return causeth the chambers to be cleansed. 10 He reformeth the offices in the house of God, 15 the violation of the sabbath, 23 and the marriages with strange wives.

13:1 On that {1}day {a}they read in the book of Moses{2} in the audience{3} of the people; and therein was found written, {b}that the Ammonite and the Moabite should not come into the congregation of God for ever;{4}
13:2 Because they {c}met not the children of Israel with bread and with water, but {d}hired Balaam against them, that he should curse them: howbeit our God {e}turned the curse into a blessing.
13:3 Now it came to pass, when they {f}had heard the law, that they separated from Israel all the {g}mixed multitude.
13:4 ¶ And before this, {h}Eliashib the priest, having the oversight{5} of the chamber of the house of our God, *was* allied unto {i}Tobiah;{6}
13:5 And he had prepared for him {k}a great chamber, where aforetime they laid the meat-offerings, the frankincense, and the vessels, and the tithes of the corn, the new wine, and the oil, ({l}which was commanded *to be given* to the Levites, and the singers, and the porters,) and the offerings of the priests.
13:6 But in all this *time* {m}was not I at Jerusalem: for {n}in the two and thirtieth year of Artaxerxes king of Babylon{7} came I unto the king, and after certain days{8} obtained{9} I leave of the king.
13:7 And I came to Jerusalem,{1} and {o}understood of the evil that Eliashib did for Tobiah, in preparing him a chamber in the {p}courts of the house of God.
13:8 And it grieved me sore; therefore {q}I cast forth all the household-stuff of Tobiah out of the chamber.
13:9 Then I commanded, and they {r}cleansed the chambers; and thither brought I again the vessels of the house of God, with the meat-offering and the frankincense.
13:10 ¶ And I perceived that {s}the portions of the Levites had not been given *them*: for the Levites and the singers, that did the work, were fled every one {t}to his field.{2}
13:11 Then {u}contended I with the rulers, and said, {x}Why is the house of God forsaken? And I gathered them{3} together, and set them in their place.{4}
13:12 Then {y}brought all Judah the tithe of the corn, and the new wine, and the oil, unto the treasuries.{5}
13:13 And I made treasurers over the treasuries, Shelemiah the priest, and Zadok the scribe; and of the Levites, Pedaiah; and next to them{6} *was* Hanan the son of Zaccur, the son of Mattaniah; for they were counted {z}faithful; and their office *was*{7} to distribute unto their brethren.
13:14 Remember{a} me, O my God, concerning this, and wipe{8} not out my good deeds{9} that I have done for the house of my God, and for the offices{1} thereof.{2}
13:15 ¶ In those days saw I in Judah {b}*some* treading wine-presses on the sabbath, and bringing in sheaves, and lading asses; as also wine, grapes, and figs, and all *manner of* burdens, which they brought into Jerusalem on the sabbath-day: and I testified *against them* in the day wherein they sold victuals.
13:16 There dwelt {c}men of Tyre also therein, which brought fish, and all manner of ware, and sold on the sabbath unto the children of Judah, and in Jerusalem.
13:17 Then I {d}contended with the nobles of Judah, and said unto them, What evil thing *is* this that ye do, and profane the sabbath-day?
13:18 Did{e} not your fathers thus, and did not our God bring all this evil upon us, and upon this city? yet ye bring more wrath{3} upon Israel by profaning the sabbath.
13:19 And it came to pass, that, when the gates of Jerusalem began to be dark before the sabbath, {f}I commanded that the gates should be shut, and charged that they should not be opened till after the sabbath: and *some* of my {4}servants set I at the gates, *that* there should no burden be brought in on the sabbath-day.
13:20 So the merchants, and sellers of all kind of ware, lodged without Jerusalem once or twice.
13:21 Then I testified against them,{5} and said unto them, Why lodge ye about the wall?{6} if ye do *so* again, I will lay hands on you. From that time forth came they no *more* on the sabbath.
13:22 And I {g}commanded the Levites, {h}that they should cleanse themselves, and *that* they should come *and* keep the gates, to sanctify the sabbath-day. {i}Remember me, O my God, *concerning* this also, and spare me according to the greatness{7} of thy mercy.
