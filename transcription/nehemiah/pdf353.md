But nothing more effectually entails misery on our offspring than profane marriages: and yet no commands of God, no warnings of Providence, are sufficient to restrain men. The sharpest rebukes, corrections, and brands of infamy, are necessary to check abominations which have once become fashionable. And it is a great mercy if, in critical conjunctures, a magistrate or minister is helped so to behave in his station, as that he may look up to God for a gracious reward.
