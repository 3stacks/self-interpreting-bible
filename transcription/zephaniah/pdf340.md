Zephaniah declares that the Lord will famish all the gods of the earth, and the heathen gods once worshiped in this beautiful city are all destroyed. From the days of the Volci, the Samnities, the Oscans, the Campanians, the Apulians, the Lucanians, the Calabians, the Bruttians, and the Siculians, till now, this region of almost unbroken summer has been the scene of intrigue and bloodshed. -->
