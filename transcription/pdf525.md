B<span class="sc">EHOLD</span>, bless ye the Lord, all ye
&emsp;that his attendants are,
Ev'n you that in God's temple be,
&emsp;and praise him nightly there.
2 Your hands within God's holy place
&emsp;lift up, and praise his name.
3 From Sion hill the Lord thee bless,
&emsp;that heav'n and earth did frame.

## Psalm CXXXV.

This Psalm contains (1) Earnest exhortations to all concerned to praise the Lord, ver. 1-3, 19-21. (2) Rich matter for praise—God considered as the God of Israel, ver. 4; as the God of gods. ver. 5; as the God of the whole world, ver. 6, 7; as a God terrible to the enemies of Israel, ver. 8-11; but kind to Israel, ver. 12-14; and as the only true and living God, before whom all others are but vanity and falsehood, ver. 15-18. ¶ Come near, my soul, and sing what <span class="sc">Jehovah</span> is, and hath done for his church, and what he is to, and hath done for me.

P<span class="sc">RAISE</span> ye the Lord, the Lord's name praise;
&emsp;his servants, praise ye God.
2 Who stand in God's house, in the courts
&emsp;of our God make abode.
3 Praise ye the Lord, for he is good;
&emsp;unto him praises sing:
&emsp;Sing praises to his name, because
&emsp;it is a pleasant thing.

4 For Jacob to himself the Lord
&emsp;did chuse of his good pleasure,
&emsp;And he hath chosen Israel
&emsp;for his peculiar treasure.
5 Because I know assuredly
&emsp;the Lord is very great,
&emsp;And that our Lord above all gods
&emsp;in glory hath his seat.

6 What thing soever pleas'd the Lord,
&emsp;that in the heav'n did he,
&emsp;And in the earth, the seas, and all
&emsp;the places deep that be.
7 He from the ends of earth doth make
&emsp;the vapours to ascend;
&emsp;With rain he lightnings makes, and wind
&emsp;doth from his treasures send.

8 Egypt's first-born, from man to beast
9 who smote. Strange tokens he
&emsp;On Phar'oh and his servants sent,
&emsp;Egypt, in midst of thee.
10 He smote great nations, slew great kings:
11 Sihon of Heshbon king,
&emsp;And Og of Bashan, and to nought
&emsp;did Canaan's kingdoms bring:

12 And for a wealthy heritage
&emsp;their pleasant land he gave,
&emsp;An heritage which Israel,
&emsp;his chosen folk, should have.
13 Thy name, O Lord, shall still endure,
&emsp;and thy memorial
&emsp;With honour shall continu'd be
&emsp;to generations all.

14 For why? the righteous God will judge
&emsp;his people righteously;
&emsp;Concerning those that do him serve,
&emsp;himself repent will he.
15 The idols of the nations
&emsp;of silver are and gold,
&emsp;And by the hands of men is made
&emsp;their fashion and mould.

16 Mouths have they, but they do not speak;
&emsp;eyes, but they do not see;
17 Ears have they, but hear not; and in
&emsp;their mouths no breathing be.
18 Their makers are like them; so are
&emsp;all that on them rely.
19 O Isr'el's house, bless God; bless God,
&emsp;O Aaron's family.

20 O bless the Lord, of Levi's house
&emsp;ye who his servants are;
&emsp;And bless the holy name of God,
&emsp;all ye the Lord that fear.
21 And blessed be the Lord our God
&emsp;from Sion's holy hill,
&emsp;Who dwelleth at Jerusalem.
&emsp;The Lord O praise ye still.

## Psalm CXXXVI.

This Psalm is a continued exhortation to praise the Lord for the perpetual displays of his mercy. The hearty singing of it has been honoured with the most signal appearances of God's kindness, 2 Ch. v. 13, and xx. 21, 22. We are in it directed to praise God (1) As great and good in himself, ver. 1-3. (2) As the Creator of all things, ver. 5-9. (3) As the God and Saviour of Israel, who brought them out of Egypt, through the Red Sea and wilderness, cast out the nations, and gave them the possession of Canaan, ver. 10-22. (4) As our gracious and condescending Redeemer, ver. 23, 24. (5) As the great and sovereign Benefactor of all creatures, ver. 25, 26. ¶ While I sing, let my heart be melted and inflamed with the loving-kindness and mercy of God in Christ; and let me behold, believe, and admire the unbounded extent and everlasting duration thereof.

G<span class="sc">IVE</span> thanks to God, for good is he:
&emsp;for mercy hath he ever.
2 Thanks to the God of gods give ye:
&emsp;for his grace faileth never.
3 Thanks give the Lord of lords unto:
&emsp;for mercy hath he ever.
4 Who only wonders great can do:
&emsp;for his grace faileth never.

5 Who by his wisdom made heav'ns high:
&emsp;for mercy hath he ever.
6 Who stretch'd the earth above the sea:
&emsp;for his grace faileth never.
7 To him that made the great lights shine:
&emsp;for mercy hath he ever.
8 The sun to rule till day decline:
&emsp;for his grace faileth never.

9 The moon and stars to rule by night:
&emsp;for mercy hath he ever.
10 Who Egypt's first-born kill'd outright:
&emsp;for his grace faileth never.
11 And Isr'el brought from Egypt land:
&emsp;for mercy hath he ever.
12 With stretch'd-out arm, and with strong hand:
&emsp;for his grace faileth never.

13 By whom the Red sea parted was:
&emsp;for mercy hath he ever.
14 And through its midst made Isr'el pass:
&emsp;for his grace faileth never.
15 But Phar'oh and his host did drown:
&emsp;for mercy hath he ever.
16 Who through the desert led his own:
&emsp;for his grace faileth never.

17 To him great kings who overthrew:
&emsp;for he hath mercy ever.
18 Yea, famous kings in battle slew:
&emsp;for his grace faileth never.
19 Ev'n Sihon king of Amorites:
&emsp;for he hath mercy ever.
20 And Og the king of Bashanites:
&emsp;for his grace faileth never.

21 Their land in heritage to have:
&emsp;(for mercy hath he ever.)
22 His servant Isr'el right he gave:
&emsp;for his grace faileth never.
23 In our low state who on us thought:
&emsp;for he hath mercy ever.
24 And from our foes our freedom wrought:
&emsp;for his grace faileth never.

25 Who doth all flesh with food relieve:
&emsp;for he hath mercy ever.
26 Thanks to the God of heaven give:
&emsp;for his grace faileth never.

P<span class="sc">RAISE</span> God, for he is kind:
&emsp;His mercy lasts for aye.
2 Give thanks with heart and mind
&emsp;To God of gods alway:
&emsp;&emsp;For certainly
&emsp;&emsp;His mercies dure
&emsp;&emsp;Most firm and sure
&emsp;&emsp;Eternally.

3 The Lord of lords praise ye,
&emsp;Whose mercies still endure.
4 Great wonders only he
&emsp;Doth work by his great pow'r:
&emsp;&emsp;For certainly, &c.

5 Which God omnipotent,
&emsp;By might and wisdom high,
&emsp;The heav'n and firmament
&emsp;Did frame, as we may see:
&emsp;&emsp;For certainly, &c.

6 To him who did outstretch
&emsp;This earth so great and wide,
&emsp;Above the waters' reach
&emsp;Making 't to abide:
&emsp;&emsp;For certainly, &c.

7 Great lights he made to be;
&emsp;For his grace lasteth aye:
&emsp;Such as the sun we see,
&emsp;To rule the lightsome day:
&emsp;&emsp;For certainly, &c.

9 Also the moon so clear,
&emsp;Which shineth in our sight;
&emsp;The stars that do appear,
&emsp;To guide the darksome night;
&emsp;&emsp;For certainly, &c.

10 To him that Egypt smote,
&emsp;Who did his message scorn;
&emsp;And in his anger hot
&emsp;Did kill all their first-born:
&emsp;&emsp;For certainly, &c.

11 Thence Isr'el out he brought;
&emsp;For his grace lasteth ever.
12 With a strong hand he wrought,
&emsp;And stretch'd-out arm deliver:
&emsp;&emsp;For certainly, &c.

13 The sea he cut in two;
&emsp;For his grace lasteth still.
14 And through its midst to go
&emsp;Made his own Israel:
&emsp;&emsp;For certainly, &c.

15 But overwhelm'd and lost
&emsp;Was proud king Pharaoh,
&emsp;With all his mighty host,
&emsp;And chariots there also:
&emsp;&emsp;For certainly, &c.

16 To him who pow'rfully
&emsp;His chosen people led,
&emsp;Ev'n through the desert dry,
&emsp;And in that place them fed:
&emsp;&emsp;For certainly, &c.

17 To him great kings who smote;
&emsp;For his grace hath no bound.
18 Who slew, and spared not
&emsp;Kings famous and renown'd:
&emsp;&emsp;For certainly, &c.

19 Sihon the Am'rites' king;
&emsp;For his grace lasteth ever:
20 Og also, who did reign
&emsp;The land of Bashan over:
&emsp;&emsp;For certainly, &c.

21 Their land by lot he gave;
&emsp;For his grace faileth never,
22 That Isr'el might it have
&emsp;In heritage for ever:
&emsp;&emsp;For certainly, &c.

23 Who hath remembered
&emsp;Us in our low estate;
24 And us delivered
&emsp;From foes which did us hate:
&emsp;&emsp;For certainly, &c.

25 Who to all flesh gives food;
&emsp;For his grace faileth never.
26 Give thanks to God most good,
&emsp;The God of heav'n, for ever:
&emsp;&emsp;For certainly, &c.
