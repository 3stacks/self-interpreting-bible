8:11+ all the things that may be desired are not to be compared to it.
8:12 ¶ I{n} wisdom dwell with prudence,{4} and find out knowledge of witty inventions.
8:13 The{o} fear of the LORD *is* to hate evil: pride,{p} and arrogancy, and the evil way, and the froward mouth, do I hate.
8:14 Counsel{q} *is* mine, and sound wisdom: I *am* understanding; {r}I have strength.{5}
8:15 ¶ By{s} me kings reign, and princes decree justice.
8:16 By me princes rule, and nobles, *even* all the judges of the earth.
8:17 I{t} love them that love me; and {u}those that seek me early shall find me.
8:18 Riches and honour are with me; yea, durable riches and righteousness.
8:19 My{x} fruit *is* better than gold, yea, than fine gold; and my revenue than choice silver.
8:20 I{y} lead{6} in the way of righteousness, in the midst of the paths of judgment:
8:21 That I may cause those that love me {z}to inherit substance; and I will fill their treasures.
8:22 ¶ The{a} LORD possessed me in the beginning of his way, before his works of old.{7}
8:23 I{b} was set up from everlasting, from the beginning, or ever the earth was.
8:24 When *there were* no depths, I was brought forth; when *there were* no fountains abounding with water.
8:25 Before{c} the mountains were settled, before the hills was I brought forth:
8:26 While as yet he had not made the earth, nor the fields,{8} nor the highest part{9} of the dust of the world.
8:27 When he {d}prepared the heavens, I *was* there; {e}when he set a compass{1} upon the face of the depth:
8:28 When he {g}established the clouds above: when he strengthened the fountains of the deep:
8:29 When{h} he gave to the sea his decree, that the waters should not pass his commandment: when he {i}appointed the foundations of the earth:
8:30 Then{j} I was by him, *as* one brought up *with him*: and I was daily *his* delight, rejoicing always before him;
8:31 Rejoicing in the {k}habitable part of his earth; and {l}my delights *were* with the sons of men.
8:32 ¶ Now therefore {n}hearken unto me, O ye children: for {o}blessed *are they that* keep my ways.
8:33 Hear{p} instruction, and be wise, and refuse it not.
8:34 Blessed{q} *is* the man that heareth me, {r}watching daily at my gates, waiting at the posts of my doors.{2}
8:35 For{s} whoso findeth me findeth life, and shall obtain{3} favour of the LORD.
8:36 But{t} he that sinneth against me wrongeth his own soul: all they that hate me love death.
