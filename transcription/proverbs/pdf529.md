16:16 How{u} much better *is it* to get wisdom than gold? and to get understanding rather to be chosen than silver?
16:17 The{v} highway of the upright *is* to depart from evil: {x}he that keepeth his way preserveth his soul.
16:18 Pride{y} *goeth* before destruction; and an haughty spirit before a fall.
16:19 Better{z} *it is to be* of an humble spirit with the lowly, than to divide the spoil with the proud.
16:20 He that handleth a matter{1} wisely shall find good; and whoso {a}trusteth in the LORD, happy *is* he.
16:21 The{b} wise in heart shall be called prudent; and the sweetness of the lips increaseth learning.
16:22 Understanding{c} *is* a well-spring of life unto him that hath it: {d}but the instruction of fools *is* folly.
16:23 The{e} heart of the wise teacheth{2} his mouth, and addeth learning to his lips.
16:24 Pleasant{g} words *are as* an honey-comb, sweet to the soul, and health to the bones.
16:25 There{h} is a way that seemeth right unto a man; but the end thereof *are* the ways of {3}death.
16:26 He that {4}laboureth, {i}laboureth for himself; for his mouth craveth it of him.{5}
16:27 An{j} ungodly man{6} diggeth up evil; and in his lips *there is* as a burning fire.
16:28 A{k} froward man soweth{7} strife; and a whisperer separateth chief friends.
16:29 A{l} violent man enticeth his neighbour, and leadeth him into the way *that is* not good.
16:30 He{n} shutteth his eyes to devise froward things; moving his lips he bringeth evil to pass.
16:31 The{o} hoary head *is* a crown of glory, *if* it be found {p}in the way of righteousness.
16:32 *He*{q} *that is* slow to anger *is* better than the mighty; {r}and he that ruleth his spirit than he that taketh a city.
