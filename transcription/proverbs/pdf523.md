11:17 The{u} merciful man doeth good to his own soul: but *he that is* cruel troubleth his own flesh.
11:18 The{v} wicked worketh a deceitful work: but to him that soweth righteousness{2} *shall be* a sure reward.
11:19 As{x} righteousness *tendeth* to life; so he that pursueth evil *pursueth it* to his own death.
11:20 They{y} that are of a froward heart *are* abomination to the LORD: but *such as are* upright in *their* way *are* his delight.
11:21 *Though*{z} hand *join* in hand, the wicked shall not be unpunished: but the seed of the righteous shall be delivered.
11:22 *As* a jewel of gold in a swine's snout, *so is* a fair woman which is without{3} discretion.
11:23 The{a} desire of the righteous *is* only good: *but* the expectation of the wicked *is* wrath.
11:24 There{b} is that scattereth, and yet increaseth; and *there is* that withholdeth more than is meet, but *it tendeth* to poverty.
11:25 The{c} liberal soul{4} shall be made fat; and he that watereth shall be watered also himself.{5}
11:26 He that withholdeth corn, the people shall curse him: but {d}blessing *shall be* upon the head of him that {e}selleth *it*.{6}
11:27 He{g} that diligently seeketh good procureth favour: {h}but he that seeketh mischief, it shall come unto him.
11:28 He{i} that trusteth in his riches shall fall: but {j}the righteous shall flourish as a branch.
11:29 He{k} that troubleth his own house shall inherit the wind: {l}and the fool *shall be* servant to the wise of heart.
11:30 The{n} fruit of the righteous *is* a tree of life; and he that {o}winneth{7} souls *is* wise.
11:31 Behold,{p} the righteous shall be recompensed in the earth: much more the wicked and the sinner.
