26:2 As the bird by wandering, as the swallow by flying; {c}so the curse causeless shall not come.
26:3 A{d} whip for the horse, a bridle for the ass, and a rod for the fool's back.
26:4 Answer{e} not a fool according to his folly,{2} lest thou also be like unto him.
26:5 Answer{g} a fool according to his folly,{3} lest he be wise in his own conceit.{4}
26:6 He{h} that sendeth a message by the hand of a fool cutteth off the feet, *and* drinketh {5}damage.{6}
26:7 The legs of the lame are not {7}equal; {i}so *is* a parable in the mouth of fools.
26:8 As he that bindeth a stone in a {8}sling, {j}so *is* he that giveth honour to a fool.{9}
26:9 *As*{k} a thorn goeth up into the hand of a drunkard, so *is* a parable in the mouth of fools.{1}
26:10 The great *God*, that formed all *things*, both{l} rewardeth the fool, and rewardeth transgressors.{2}
26:11 As{n} a dog returneth to his vomit; *so* a fool returneth to his folly.{3}
26:12 Seest{o} thou a man wise in his own conceit? *there is* more hope of a fool than of him.
26:13 The{p} slothful *man* saith, *There is* a lion in the way, a lion *is* in the streets.
26:14 *As*{q} the door turneth upon his hinges; so *doth* the slothful upon his bed.
26:15 The{r} slothful hideth his hand in *his* bosom; it grieveth him{4} to bring it again to his mouth.{5}
26:16 The{s} sluggard *is* wiser in his own conceit than seven men that can render a reason.
26:17 He{t} that passeth by, *and* meddleth with strife *belonging* not to him, *is like* one that taketh a dog by the ears.{6}
26:18 As a mad *man* who casteth {7}firebrands, arrows, and death,
26:19 So{u} *is* the man *that* deceiveth his neighbour, and saith, Am not I in sport?
26:20 Where{8} no wood is, *there* the fire goeth out; so {v}where *there is* no tale-bearer,{9} the strife ceaseth.{1}
26:21 *As*{x} coals *are* to burning coals, and wood to fire; so *is* a contentious man to kindle strife.
26:22 The{y} words of a tale-bearer *are* as wounds, and they go down into the innermost parts{2} of the belly.
26:23 Burning{z} lips,{3} and a wicked heart, *are like* a potsherd covered with silver dross.
26:24 He that hateth dissembleth{4} with his lips, and layeth up deceit within him:
26:25 When{a} he speaketh fair,{5} believe him not; for *there are* seven abominations{6} in his heart.
26:26 *Whose* {7}hatred{b} is covered by deceit, his wickedness shall be showed before the *whole* congregation.{8}
26:27 Whoso{c} diggeth a pit shall fall therein; and he that rolleth a stone, it will return upon him.
26:28 A{d} lying tongue hateth *those that are* afflicted by it; {e}and a flattering mouth worketh ruin.
