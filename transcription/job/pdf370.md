Ibn er Rabi, as quoted by Lawrence Oliphant in his Land of Gilead, says: "To the prophets buried in the region of Damascus belong Job, and his tomb is near Nawa in the district of Hauran." The chief place is Nawa, rich in wheat and other cereals." The Hauran is the store-house of Damascus. It is a highly productive and thickly populated region. The artist who took these pictures and the writer were in Damascus when the first train went out from Damascus seventy miles into the Hauran.
