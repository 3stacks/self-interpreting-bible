<!-- text -->
28:3 He setteth an end to darkness,{2} and {d}searcheth out all perfection; the stones of darkness, and the shadow of death.
28:4 The flood breaketh out from the inhabitant; *even the waters* {e}forgotten of the foot: they are dried up, they are gone away from men.{3}
28:5 *As for* the earth, {f}out of it cometh bread; and under it is turned up as it were {g}fire.
28:6 The stones of it *are* the place of {h}sapphires; and it hath dust of gold.{4}
28:7 *There*{i} *is* a path{5} which no fowl knoweth, and which the vulture's eye hath not seen:
28:8 The lion's whelps have not trodden it, nor the fierce lion passed by it.
28:9 He putteth forth his hand upon the {k}rock; he overturneth the mountains by the roots.
28:10 He {l}cutteth out rivers among the rocks; and his eye seeth every precious thing.
28:11 He bindeth the floods {m}from overflowing; and *the thing that is* hid bringeth he forth to light.
28:12 But where shall {n}wisdom be found? and where *is* the place of understanding?
28:13 Man {o}knoweth not the price thereof; neither is it {p}found in the land of the living.
28:14 The {q}depth saith, It *is* not in me; and the sea saith, *It is* not with me.
28:15 It {r}cannot be gotten for gold,{6} neither shall silver be weighed *for* the price thereof.
28:16 It cannot be valued with {s}the gold of Ophir, with the precious onyx, or the sapphire.
28:17 The gold and the crystal cannot equal it: and the exchange of it *shall not be for* jewels of find gold.{7}
28:18 No mention shall be made of {t}coral, or of pearls: for the price of wisdom *is* above rubies.
28:19 The topaz{8} of Ethiopia{9} shall not equal it, neither shall it be valued with pure gold.
28:20 Whence{u} then cometh wisdom? and where *is* the place of understanding?
28:21 Seeing {x}it is hid from the eyes of all living, and kept close from the fowls of the air.{1}
28:22 Destruction{y} and death say,{2} We have heard the fame thereof with our ears.
28:23 God{z} understandeth the way thereof, and he knoweth the place thereof.
