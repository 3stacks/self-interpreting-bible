<!-- running: Bildad showeth God's justice. | JOB VIII. | The certain destruction of the hypocrite. -->

<!-- chapter: 8 -->
<!-- summary -->
1 Bildad showeth God's justice in dealing with men according to their works. 8 He appealeth to antiquity to prove the certain destruction of the hypocrite. 20 He applieth the argument of God's just dealing to the case of Job.

<!-- text -->
8:1 Then answered Bildad{1} the Shuhite, and said.
8:2 How long wilt thou speak these *things*? and *how long shall* the words of thy mouth {a}*be like* a strong wind?
8:3 Doth{b} God pervert judgment? or doth the Almighty pervert justice?
8:4 If thy children have sinned {c}against him, and he have cast them away for {2}their transgression;{3}
8:5 If{d} thou wouldest seek unto God betimes, and make thy supplication to the Almighty;
8:6 If{e} thou *wert* pure and upright, surely now he would awake for thee, and make the habitation of thy righteousness prosperous.
8:7 Though{f} thy beginning was small,{4} yet thy latter end should greatly increase.
8:8 For inquire, {g}I pray thee, of the former age, and prepare thyself to the search of their fathers:
8:9 (For {h}we *are but of* yesterday,{5} and know nothing, because our days upon earth *are* a shadow:)
8:10 Shall not they {i}teach thee, *and* tell thee, and utter words out of their heart?
8:11 Can the rush grow up without mire? can the flag grow without water?
8:12 Whilst{k} it *is* yet in his greenness, *and* not cut down, it withereth before any *other* herb:
8:13 So *are* the {l}paths of all that forget God; and the {m}hypocrite's hope shall perish:{7}
8:14 Whose hope shall be cut off, and whose trust *shall be* a spider's {n}web.
8:15 He shall {o}lean upon his house,{8} but it shall not stand; he shall hold it fast, but it shall not endure.
8:16 He{p} *is* green before the sun, and his branch shooteth forth in his garden.
8:17 His roots are wrapped about the heap, *and* seeth the place of stones.{9}
8:18 If{q} he destroy him from his place, then *it* shall deny him, *saying*, I have not seen thee.{1}
8:19 Behold, {r}this *is* the joy of his way,{2} and {s}out of the earth shall others grow.
8:20 Behold, God {t}will not cast away a perfect man, neither will he {u}help the evil-doers; >>
