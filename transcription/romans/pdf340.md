14:11 For it is written, {p}*As* I live, saith the Lord, every knee shall bow to me, and every tongue shall confess to God.
14:12 So then {q}every one of us shall give account of himself to God.
14:13 Let{r} us not therefore judge one another any more: but judge this rather, {s}that no man put a stumbling-block or an occasion to fall in his brother's way.
14:14 I{t} know, and am persuaded by the Lord Jesus, that *there is* nothing unclean{7} of itself: but to him that esteemeth any thing to be unclean,{7} to him *it is* unclean.
14:15 But if thy brother be grieved with *thy* meat, now walkest thou {u}not charitably.{8} Destroy not{9} him with thy meat, {v}for whom Christ died.
14:16 Let{x} not then your good{1} be evil spoken of:
14:17 For{y} the kingdom of God is not meat and drink;{2} but {z}righteousness, and peace, and joy in the Holy Ghost.
14:18 For{a} he that in these things serveth Christ *is* acceptable to God, and approved of men.
14:19 Let{b} us therefore follow after the things which make for peace, and things wherewith one may edify another.
14:20 For{c} meat destroy not {d}the work of God. All things indeed *are* pure; but *it is* evil for that man who eateth with offence.
14:21 *It*{e} is good neither to eat flesh, nor to drink wine, nor *any thing* whereby thy brother stumbleth, or is offended, or is made weak.
14:22 Hast thou {g}faith? have *it* to thyself before God. {h}Happy *is* he that condemneth not himself in that thing which he alloweth.
14:23 And he that doubteth{3} is damned if he eat, because *he eateth* not of faith: for {i}whatsoever *is* not of faith is sin.

15:1 We{a} then that are strong{1} ought to bear{2} the infirmities of the weak, and not to please ourselves.
15:2 Let{b} every one of us please *his* neighbour for *his* good to edification.
15:3 For{c} even Christ pleased not himself; but, as it is written, The reproaches of them that reproached thee fell on me.
15:4 For{d} whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope.
15:5 Now{e} the God of patience and consolation grant you to be {g}like-minded one toward another, according to{3} Christ Jesus;
15:6 That ye may with one mind *and* one mouth glorify God, even the {h}Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.
