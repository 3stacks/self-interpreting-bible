But whatever refuge sinners flee to, it will be found incapable to protect them. God will take care that his providence shall exactly correspond with his purpose and word. ## Chapter XV.
[Ver. 19. It appears from the narrative that the kings of Israel had been accustomed to acknowledge in some form the supremacy of the rulers of Assyria. Menahem neglected to do so; and perhaps proceeded to a more overt act of defiance in capturing Tiphsah, on the very borders of Assyria. Thus provoked, the Assyrian monarch marched against Palestine, and the kingdom was saved only by the payment of an enormous ransom. *P.*]
