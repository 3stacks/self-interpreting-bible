## Chapter XX.
**Reflections.**—Neither greatness nor goodness can exempt from sickness or death. Not a moment of life but the present is certain. And often God lays men aside from their work when we think there is most need of them. But, oh! the vast preparation of state, frame, and exercise that is necessary for a comfortable entrance into eternity! There is need for ministers to inculcate this in the most earnest and solemn manner.
