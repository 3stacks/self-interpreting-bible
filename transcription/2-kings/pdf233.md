The interior of the temple that stood where the Mosque of Omar now stands was richly furnished. So the interior of this Mohammedan place of worship is most elaborately furnished. The building has 8 sides, each 68 feet long, the whole being covered with richly colored porcelain tiles, and a frieze of tiles running around the building upon which are written passages from the Koran. The great dome is supported by Corinthian columns. There are 56 stained glass windows of great brilliancy and beauty. The dome is 98 feet high, and 66 in diameter. -->
