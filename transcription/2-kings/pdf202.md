Dothan lay upon the highway which the Ishmaelite merchants were compelled to travel, and it was during one of their regular marches along this way that they bought Joseph from his brethren, as we are told in Genesis xxxvii:17. Here we know it was that the Lord opened the eyes of Elisha's young man, because when the King of Syria warred against Israel, he wanted to know of the whereabouts of Elisha, the man of God.
