16:1 In the seventeenth year of Pekah the son of Remaliah, Ahaz the son of Jotham king of Judah began to reign.
16:2 Twenty{a} years old *was* Ahaz{1} when he began to reign, and reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem, and did not *that which was* right in the sight of the LORD his God, like David his father.
16:3 But he walked in the way of the kings of Israel, yea, and made his son to pass through the fire,{2} according to the abominations of the heathen, whom the LORD cast out from before the children of Israel.
16:4 And he sacrificed and burnt incense in the high places, and on the hills, and under every green tree.{3}
16:5 ¶ Then{b} Rezin king of Syria, and Pekah son of Remaliah king of Israel came up to Jerusalem to war: and they besieged Ahaz, but could not overcome *him*.
16:6 At that time Rezin king of Syria recovered Elath{c} to Syria, and drave the Jews{4} from Elath: and the Syrians came to Elath, and dwelt there unto this day.{5}
16:7 ¶ So Ahaz sent messengers to {d}Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria, saying, I *am* thy servant and thy son: come up, and save me out of the hand of the king of Syria, and out of the hand of the king of Israel, which rise up against me.
16:8 And {e}Ahaz took the silver and gold *that was* found in the house of the LORD, and in the treasures of the king's house, and sent *it for* a present to the king of Assyria.{6}
16:9 And the king of Assyria hearkened unto him: for the king of Assyria {f}went up against Damascus, and took it, and carried *the people of* it captive to {g}Kir,{7} and slew Rezin.
16:10 ¶ And king Ahaz went to Damascus {h}to meet Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria, and {i}saw an altar{8} that *was* at Damascus: and king Ahaz sent to Urijah the priest the fashion of the altar, and the pattern of it, according to all the workmanship thereof.
16:11 And {k}Urijah the priest built an altar according to all that king Ahaz had sent from Damascus: so Urijah the priest made *it* against king Ahaz{9} came from Damascus.
16:12 And when the king was come from Damascus, {l}the king saw the altar: and the king approached to the altar, and offered thereon.
16:13 And {m}he burnt his burnt-offering and his meat-offering, and poured his drink-offering, and sprinkled the blood of his{1} peace-offerings, upon the altar.
16:14 And he brought also {n}the brazen altar, which *was* before the LORD, from the forefront of the house, from between the altar and the house of the LORD, and put it on the north{2} side of the altar.{3}
16:15 And king Ahaz commanded Urijah the priest, saying, Upon the {o}great altar burn the morning burnt-offering, and the evening meat-offering, and the king's burnt-sacrifice, and his meat-offering, with the burnt-offering of all the people of the land, and their meat-offering, and their drink-offerings; and sprinkle upon it all the blood of the burnt-offering, and all the blood of the sacrifice: and the brazen altar shall be for me to {p}inquire *by*.{4}
16:16 Thus did Urijah the priest, {q}according to all that king Ahaz commanded.
16:17 ¶ And king Ahaz {r}cut off the borders of the bases, and removed the laver from off them; and took down the sea from off the brazen oxen that *were* under it, and put it upon a pavement of stones:
16:18 And the covert{5} for the sabbath{6} that they had built in the house, and the king's entry without, turned he from the house of the LORD for the king of Assyria.{7}
16:19 ¶ Now{s} the rest of the acts of Ahaz which he did, *are* they not written in the book of the Chronicles of the kings of Judah?
16:20 And Ahaz slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in the city of David: and Hezekiah his son reigned in his stead.
