## Chapter XV.
[Ver. 27. In the parallel passage in 2 Sa. 6. 14, instead of the words 'And David was clothed with a robe of fine linen,' we find, 'And David danced with all his might.' In the Hebrew there is such a close similarity in letters and sound between the two statements that some commentators think the text in Chronicles is a corruption, especially as in the last clause it is said, 'David had upon him an ephod of linen,' and in ver. 29 reference is made to Michal having seen David dancing. As there is no MS. authority for the alleged corruption I feel bound to reject it. *P.*]
