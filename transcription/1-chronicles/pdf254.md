## Chapter XIV.
[Ver. 8. *The Philistines went up to seek David.* Why did they wait so long during David's weakness and contests with the family of Saul? Hitherto as the head of a weak, divided, and self-wasted kingdom, they did not fear him. They perhaps had politically fomented the national strife, or encouraged the weaker against the stronger party, in hopes of the ruin of both by mutual wounds; but now that all parties have united in favour of David, the Philistines are roused to a sense of danger, and assemble to crush him before he could be supposed able to concentrate his forces. *C.*]
