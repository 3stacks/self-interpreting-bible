8:20 The harvest is past,{2} the summer is ended, and we are not saved.
8:21 For{p} the hurt of the daughter of my people am I hurt; I am black; astonishment hath taken hold on me.
8:22 *Is there* no {q}balm in Gilead? {r}*is there* no physician there? why then is not the health of the daughter of my people recovered?{3}

<!-- summary -->
1 Jeremiah lamenteth the Jews for their manifold sins, 9 and for their judgments. 12 Disobedience is the cause of their bitter calamity. 17 He exhorteth to mourn for their destruction, 23 and to trust not in themselves, but in God. 25 He threateneth both Jews and Gentiles.

<!-- text -->
9:1 Oh that{a} my head{1} were waters, and mine eyes a fountain of tears, that I might weep day and night for the slain of the daughter of my people!
9:2 Oh{b} that I had in the wilderness a lodging place of wayfaring men, that I might leave my people, and go from them! for they *be* all adulterers, an assembly of treacherous men.
9:3 And{c} they bend their tongues *like* their bow *for* lies; but they are not valiant for the truth upon the earth: for they {d}proceed from evil to evil, and they {e}know not me, saith the LORD.
9:4 Take{g} ye heed every one of his neighbour,{2} and trust ye not in any brother: {h}for every brother will utterly supplant, and every neighbour {i}will walk with slanders.
9:5 And{j} they will deceive{3} every one his neighbour, and will not speak the truth: they have taught their tongue to speak lies, *and* {k}weary themselves to commit iniquity.
9:6 Thine{l} habitation *is* in the midst of deceit; through deceit they {n}refuse to know me, saith the LORD.
9:7 Therefore thus saith the LORD of hosts,{4} Behold,{o} I will melt them,{5} and try them; for how{p} shall I do for the daughter of my people?
9:8 Their{q} tongue *is as* an arrow shot out;{6} it speaketh deceit: *one* {r}speaketh peaceably to his neighbour with his mouth, but in heart{7} he layeth his wait.{8}
9:9 ¶ Shall{s} I not visit them for these *things?* saith the LORD: shall not my soul be avenged on such a nation as this?
9:10 For{t} the mountains will I take up a weeping and wailing, and for the {9}habitations{1} of the wilderness a lamentation, because they are burned up,{2} so that none can pass through *them;* neither can *men* hear the voice of the cattle: {3}both the fowl of the heavens and the beast are fled; they are gone.
9:11 And I {u}will make Jerusalem heaps, *and* a den of dragons; and I will make the cities of Judah {4}desolate,{5} without an inhabitant.
9:12 ¶ Who{v} *is* the wise man, that may understand this? and *who is he* to whom the mouth of the LORD hath spoken, that he may declare {w}it, for what the land perisheth *and* is burned up like a wilderness, that none passeth through?
9:13 And the LORD saith, {x}Because they have forsaken my law which I set before them, and have not obeyed my voice, neither walked therein;
9:14 But have walked after the {6}imagination of their own heart, and after Baalim, which their fathers taught them:
9:15 Therefore{y} thus saith the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel; Behold, {z}I will feed them, *even* this people, with wormwood, and give them water of gall{7} to drink.
9:16 I will {a}scatter them also among the heathen, whom neither they nor their fathers have known: and I will send a sword after them, till I have consumed{8} them.
9:17 ¶ Thus saith the LORD of hosts, {b}Consider ye, and call for the {c}mourning women, that they may come; and send for {9}cunning *women,* that they may come:
9:18 And let them make haste, and take up a wailing for us, that our eyes may run down with tears, and our eyelids gush out with waters.
9:19 For {d}a voice of wailing is heard out of Zion, How are we spoiled! we are greatly confounded, because we have forsaken the land, because our dwellings have {e}cast *us* out.{1}
9:20 Yet hear the word of the LORD, {g}O ye women, and let your ear receive the word of his mouth, {h}and teach your daughters wailing, and every one her neighbour lamentation:{2}
9:21 For {i}death is come up into our {3}windows, *and* is entered into our palaces, to cut off the children from without, *and* the young men from the streets.
9:22 Speak, Thus saith the LORD, {j}Even the carcases of men shall fall as dung upon the open field, and as the handful after the harvestman,{4} and none shall gather *them.*
9:23 ¶ Thus saith the LORD, {k}Let not the wise *man* glory in his wisdom, neither let the mighty *man* glory in his might, let not the rich *man* glory in his riches:
9:24 But {l}let him that glorieth glory in this, that he understandeth and knoweth me, that I *am* the LORD which {n}exercise loving-kindness, judgment, and righteousness, in the earth: for in these *things* {o}I delight, saith the LORD.
9:25 ¶ Behold,{p} the days come, saith the LORD, that {q}I will punish{5} all *them which are* {r}circumcised with the uncircumcised;
9:26 Egypt,{s} and Judah, and Edom, and the children of Ammon, and Moab,{6} and all *that are* in the utmost corners,{7} that dwell in the wilderness; for all *these* nations *are* uncircumcised, and all the house of Israel *are* {t}uncircumcised in the heart.

## Chapter X.
Ver. 17-22. Bring all your goods out of the country, where they are exposed to plunder, into the strongholds; for quickly will I, in a furious manner, drive out the inhabitants of this land by death and captivity. Alas! almost insupportable is my grief, that the whole Jewish church and nation should be quite unhinged, and none able in the least to help them!
