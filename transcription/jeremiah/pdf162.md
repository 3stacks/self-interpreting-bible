[Jeremiah, lii:25-26.]—In the Scripture above referred to, we have an account of the taking of Seraiah, the chief priest, and Zephaniah, the second priest, and others found in the city of Jerusalem, to the king of Babylon at Riblah. Damascus is upon the direct route between Jerusalem and Riblah, and the prisoners above referred to passed this city before reaching Riblah, where they were smitten and put to death by the king of Babylon. The illustration we give above is of a scene witnessed by the writer. The building to the right is the city prison. Five hundred men confined here for various offenses had mutinied, and were being shot to death from the top of the prison by the guards. We witnessed the whole scene from the veranda of our hotel which stood just across the Abana river from the prison. Several men were killed before the mutiny was quelled. -->
