## Psalm XI.
Ver. 3. If the foundations be destroyed, *i.e.* if all regard to the laws of God, all regard to order and justice, be gone, what safety can the most innocent or virtuous expect!

Ver. 6. Snares, fire, and brimstone, &c., signify all the terrible and unavoidable judgments which God executes upon the wicked in time and eternity; such as he executed upon Saul and his agents.
