## Psalm CXIV.
**Reflections.**—In infinite tenderness God chooses all who accept Christ to be his peculiar people, and to be blessed with the Spirit's witness, and gracious influences. And nothing can detain his people in their spiritual bondage if they are faithful. Nothing, no not seas of tribulation, nor mountains of unbelief and corruption, can stop their passage to the heavenly Canaan. Nor while Jesus, our smitten Rock of ages, continues a *fountain open*, shall they want refreshment by the way. Tremble then, my carnal heart, at the presence of a God in Christ as my own. And let all my hardened powers be melted by his love.

## Psalm CXV.
Ver. 8. As really destitute of the use of eyes, &c., as the idols are of the possession of these organs. No man can have the use of his eyes who looks up to the heavens and attributes their formation to unintelligible and unintelligent chance, or a carved block of marble or wood. Indeed, did idolatry live merely in history, and were it not still an extensive and powerful system, it would be scarcely possible to conceive it ever had existed as described. But it lives, while Satan, its inventor, rules; and will not fall till his kingdom is overturned. *C.*
