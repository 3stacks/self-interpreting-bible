108:2 Awake, {c}psaltery and harp; I *myself* will awake early.
108:3 I {d}will praise thee, O LORD, among the people; and I will sing praises unto thee among the nations.
108:4 For{e} thy mercy *is* great above the heavens; and thy truth *reacheth* unto the clouds.{3}
108:5 Be{g} thou exalted, O God, above the heavens, and thy glory above all the earth;{4}
108:6 That{h} thy beloved may be delivered: {i}save *with* thy right hand, and answer me.
108:7 God{j} hath spoken in his holiness; I will rejoice, I will {k}divide Shechem, and mete out the valley of {l}Succoth.
108:8 Gilead{n} *is* mine; Manasseh *is* mine; Ephraim also *is* the {o}strength of mine head; {p}Judah *is* my lawgiver;
108:9 Moab{q} *is* my wash-pot; over Edom will I cast out my shoe; over Philistia will I triumph.
108:10 Who{r} will bring me into the strong city? who will lead me into Edom?
108:11 *Wilt* not *thou*, O God, *who* {s}hast cast us off? and wilt not thou, O God, {t}go forth with our hosts?
108:12 Give{u} us help from trouble; for {v}vain *is* the help of man.
108:13 Through{x} God we shall do valiantly: {y}for he *it is that* shall tread down our enemies.

title: To the chief musician, A psalm of David.
109:1 Hold {a}not thy peace, O {b}God of my praise;
109:2 For{c} the mouth of the wicked, and the mouth of the deceitful,{1} are opened against me;{2} they{d} have spoken against me with a lying tongue.
109:3 They compassed me about also with words of hatred; and fought against me {e}without a cause.
109:4 For{g} my love they are my adversaries: {h}but I *give myself unto* prayer.{3}
109:5 And{i} they have rewarded me evil for good, and hatred for my love.
109:6 Set thou a wicked man over him; and let Satan{4} stand{j} at his right hand.{5}
109:7 When he shall be judged, let him be condemned;{6} and {k}let his prayer become sin.
109:8 Let{l} his days be few; *and* let another take his office.{7}
109:9 Let{n} his children be fatherless, and his wife a widow.
109:10 Let{o} his children be continually vagabonds, and beg; let them seek *their bread* also out of their desolate places.
109:11 Let{p} the extortioner catch all that he hath; and let the stranger spoil his labour.
109:12 Let there be none to extend mercy unto him: neither let there be any to favour his fatherless children.
109:13 Let{q} his posterity be cut off; *and* in the generation following let their name be blotted out.
109:14 Let{r} the iniquity of his fathers be remembered with the LORD; and let not the sin of his mother be blotted out.
109:15 Let{s} them be before the LORD continually, that he may {t}cut off the memory of them from the earth:
109:16 Because{u} that he remembered not to show mercy, but persecuted the poor and needy man, that he might even slay {v}the broken in heart.
109:17 As{x} he loved cursing, so let it come unto him; as he delighted not in blessing, so let it be far from him.
109:18 As he clothed himself with cursing like as with his garment, so let it come into his bowels{8} like water, and like oil into his bones.
109:19 Let{y} it be unto him as the garment *which* covereth him, and for a girdle wherewith he is girded continually.
109:20 *Let*{z} this *be* the reward of mine adversaries from the LORD, and of them that speak evil against my soul.
109:21 But{a} do thou for me,{9} O GOD the Lord, for thy name's sake: because thy mercy *is* good, deliver thou me.
109:22 For{b} I *am* poor and needy, and my heart is wounded within me.
109:23 I{c} am gone like the shadow when it declineth; I am {d}tossed up and down as the locust.
109:24 My {e}knees are weak through fasting; and my flesh faileth of fatness.
109:25 I{g} became also a reproach unto them: *when* they looked upon me they shaked their heads.
109:26 Help{h} me, O LORD my God: {i}O save me according to thy mercy:
109:27 That{j} they may know that this *is* thy hand; *that* thou, LORD, hast done it.
109:28 Let{k} them curse, but bless thou: when they arise, let them be ashamed; but let thy servant rejoice.
109:29 Let{l} mine adversaries be clothed with shame, and let them cover themselves with their own confusion, as with a mantle.
109:30 I{n} will greatly praise the LORD with my mouth; yea, I will praise him among the multitude.
