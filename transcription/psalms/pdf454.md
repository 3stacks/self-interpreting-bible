[Psalms,, lxviii:27.]—"There is little Benjamin with their ruler, the princes of Judah and their council, the princes of Zebulun, and the princes of Naphtali." Tiberias was a city of Zebulun, and was formerly occupied, according to Joshua, xix:35, by Rakkath, and is also near the hot baths referred to in Joshua, xix:35 as Hamath. The walls of the city were rebuilt by Justinian. The city was captured by the Persians under Khosroes in 614 B. C. In 1738 Dhahr-el-'Amr built a fort on the hill north of Tiberias and repaired the walls of the city, which suffered terrible damage through the earthquake of 1837. There are now in Tiberias about six thousand people, four thousand of whom are Jews, three hundred are Christians and the rest Moslems. This is one of the four sacred cities of the Jews in Palestine. -->
