<!-- chapter: 73 -->
<!-- summary -->
1 The psalmist, prevailing in a temptation, 2 showeth the occasion thereof, the prosperity of the wicked. 13 The wound given thereby, diffidence. 15 The victory over it, knowledge of God's purpose, in destroying of the wicked, and sustaining the righteous.

title: A psalm of{1} Asaph.
73:1 Truly{2} God{a} *is* good to Israel, *even* to such as are of a {b}clean heart.{3}
73:2 But as for me, {c}my feet were almost gone, my steps had well nigh slipped.{4}
73:3 For{d} I was envious{5} at the foolish, *when* I saw the prosperity of the wicked:
73:4 For{e} *there are* no bands in their death; but their strength *is* {6}firm.{7}
73:5 They{g} *are* not in trouble *as other* men;{8} neither are they plagued like{9} *other* men:
73:6 Therefore{h} pride compasseth them about as a chain; violence covereth them *as* a garment.
73:7 Their{i} eyes stand out with fatness: they have more than heart could wish.{1}
73:8 They{j} are corrupt, and speak wickedly *concerning* oppression: they speak loftily.
73:9 They {k}set their mouth against the heavens, and their tongue walketh through the earth.
73:10 Therefore his people {l}return hither; and waters{n} of a full *cup* are wrung out to them.
73:11 And they say,{2} How{o} doth God know? and is there knowledge in the most High?
73:12 Behold,{p} these *are* the ungodly, who prosper in the world; they increase *in* riches.
73:13 Verily{q} I have cleansed my heart *in* vain, and washed my hands in innocency.{3}
73:14 For{r} all the day long have I been plagued, and chastened{4} every morning.
73:15 If I say, I will speak thus; behold, {s}I should offend *against* the generation of thy children.
73:16 When{t} I thought to know this, it *was* too painful{5} for me,{6}
73:17 Until{u} I went into the sanctuary of God; *then* understood I their end.
73:18 Surely{v} thou didst set them in slippery places; thou castedst them down into destruction.
73:19 How{x} are they *brought* into desolation, as in a moment! they are utterly consumed with terrors.
73:20 As{y} a dream when *one* awaketh; *so*, O LORD, when thou awakest, thou shalt despise their image.{7}
73:21 Thus{z} my heart was grieved, and I was pricked in my reins.
73:22 So{a} foolish *was* I, and {8}ignorant:{9} I was *as* a beast{1} before thee.{2}
73:23 Nevertheless{b} I *am* continually with thee: thou hast holden *me* by my right hand.
73:24 Thou{c} shalt guide me with thy counsel, and afterward receive me *to* glory.
73:25 Whom{d} have I in heaven *but thee?* and *there is* none upon earth *that* I desire besides thee.
73:26 My{e} flesh and my heart faileth: *but* God *is* the strength{3} of my heart, and {g}my portion for ever.
73:27 For, lo, {h}they that are far from thee shall perish: thou hast destroyed all them that {i}go a whoring from thee.
73:28 But {j}*it is* good for me to draw near to God: {k}I have put my trust in the Lord GOD, that I may declare all thy works.{4}

1 The psalmist complaineth of the desolation of the sanctuary. 10 He moveth God to help in consideration of his power, 18 of his reproachful enemies, of his children, and of his covenant.

title: Maschil of Asaph.{1}
74:1 O God, {a}why hast thou cast *us* off for ever? {b}*why* doth thine anger smoke against {c}the sheep of thy pasture?
74:2 Remember {d}thy congregation, *which* thou hast purchased of old; the rod{2} of thine inheritance,{3} *which* thou hast redeemed; this {e}mount Zion, wherein thou hast dwelt.
74:3 Lift{g} up thy feet{4} unto the perpetual desolations; *even* all {h}*that* the enemy hath done wickedly in the sanctuary.{5}
74:4 Thine{i} enemies roar in the midst of thy congregations; they set up their ensigns *for* signs.{6}
74:5 *A man* was famous according as he {j}had lifted up axes upon the thick trees.
74:6 But now they break down {k}the carved work thereof at once with axes and hammers.
74:7 They have cast fire into thy {7}sanctuary; they have defiled *by casting down* the dwelling-place of thy name to the ground.
74:8 They said in their hearts, {l}Let us {8}destroy them together: they have burnt up all the {n}synagogues of God in the land.
74:9 We{o} see not our signs:{9} *there is* no more any prophet; neither *is there* among us any that knoweth how long.
74:10 O God, {p}how long shall the adversary reproach? shall the enemy blaspheme thy name for ever?
74:11 Why{q} withdrawest thou thy hand, even thy right hand? pluck *it* out of thy bosom.
74:12 For{r} God *is* my King{1} of old, working salvation in the midst of the earth.
74:13 Thou{s} didst divide{2} the sea by thy strength: thou {t}brakest the heads of the dragons{3} in the waters.
74:14 Thou brakest the heads of leviathan in pieces, *and* gavest him *to be* {u}meat to the people inhabiting the wilderness.{4}
74:15 Thou {v}didst cleave the fountain{5} and the flood: {x}thou driedst up mighty rivers.{6}
