# Period of the Patriarchs

*From the Promise to the Exodus (420 Years).*

|---|---|
| 1910 | Abraham 86 years old. Ishmael is born, Gen. xvi. |
| 1897 | Abraham 99 years old. The covenant renewed; circumcision ordained, Gen. xvii. The visit of the Three Angels, xviii. Destruction of Sodom, xix. Abraham journeys southward; second denial of Sarah, xx. (in Gerar). |
| 1896 | Isaac is born (in Beer-sheba), Gen. xxi. Long sojourn in the land of the Philistines, ver. 34. Abraham offers up Isaac, xxii. |
| 1859 | The time is not specified; the next event is the death of Sarah, aged 127 years, Abraham 137 years old, xxiii. |

| 1773 | Ishmael dies, aged 137 years, Gen. xxv. 17. |

| 1753 | Jacob, having served fourteen years, marries Leah and Rachel, Gen. xxix. 20-30. Leah bears Reuben, Simeon, Levi and Judah. Bilhah bears Dan and Naphtali to Rachel. Zilpah bears Gad and Asher to Leah. |
| 1745 | Leah bears Issachar, Zebulon and Dinah. |
| 1743 | Rachel bears Joseph, Gen. xxx. 25. Jacob serves six years for his cattle, twenty years in all, xxxi. 41. |

| 1737 | Between this year and 1825 Jacob is settled at Hebron. |
