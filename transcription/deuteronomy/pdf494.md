## Chapter V.
[Ver. 21. The varieties of expression used, in relating the same thing, by 'holy men of God,' who spoke as they were moved by 'the Holy Ghost,' is never without some important use, that will sooner or later be found out. A remarkable instance occurs in this verse. In many of the catechisms and other books authorized by *Romanists*, the *second commandment*, forbidding image worship, is omitted; but to make up the number *ten*, the tenth commandment is divided into *two*. The *transposition*, however, in the text demonstrates the invalidity of the division, and the folly as well as sin of adding to or diminishing from the words of the Lord. *C.*]
