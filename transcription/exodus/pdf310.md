<!-- illustration: EAST FROM THE PYRAMIDS OF GIZEH—A SCENE IN THE LAND WHERE THE CHILDREN OF ISRAEL SPENT 430 YEARS. [Exodus, iv:19.]—"And the Lord said unto Moses in Midian, Go, return into Egypt: for all the men are dead which sought thy life." To understand men we must study the land in which they grew up. The relation of Moses to the children of Israel, to the Exodus, and the civilization which the Hebrew people developed, was such that we cannot do better than consider him through Egypt, in which he spent the first forty years of his life. The fortunes of Israel were committed to the keeping almost wholly of Moses. Hence we shall illustrate his career by pictures of Egypt. Standing at the pyramids and looking east, we see the direction the children of Israel took when they left the land in which they had sojourned four hundred and thirty years. We see the back of the Sphinx, and in the distance in dim outline, trees which skirt the banks of the Nile. -->
