## Chapter XXVII.
**Reflections.**—Since I have the once debased the all-atoning, all-perfect, powerful, and all-accessible Jesus for my altar;—since I have his church and ordinances for a court and place of worship, divinely bounded and protected;—the oracles, gifts, and graces of his Spirit, for my all-illuminating oil;—let me, conscious of crimes unnumbered, flee to him as my refuge, and by faith lay hold on his horns of saving power and authority.
