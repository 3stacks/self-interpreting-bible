Ver. 11. Even in the Egyptians' haughty and insolent detaining of his people Israel, imagining it impossible for their God to deliver them, he showed *himself* infinitely *their* superior in wisdom and might.

Ver. 12. They feasted *before God*, near to the symbol of his presence in the cloud, or on the mount, and in holy awe of him, and as in his sight.
