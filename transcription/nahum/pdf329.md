[Nahum, ii:13.]—"Behold, I am against thee, saith the Lord of hosts, and I will burn her chariots in the smoke, and the sword shall devour the young lions; and I will cut off thy prey from the earth, and the voice of thy messengers shall no more be heard." This prophecy was uttered with reference to Nineveh, but in a general sense it may be said to be true that the Lord is against all wicked cities and sooner or later will cut them off. We give above a picture of the public baths in Pompeii. These baths are bounded on two sides by doric porticoes. Under the porticoes the bathers waited their turn, somewhat as gentlemen wait their turn in popular barber shops to-day. Amusements were advertised here. An old inscription was found on the wall of this portico after the excavation of the baths which, when translated, read as follows: "At the dedication of the baths, at the expense of Caucus Alleuis Nigidius Maius, there will be a venatio, athletic contests, sprinkling of perfumes and ointments.
