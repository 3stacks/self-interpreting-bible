## Psalm CXLV.

*David's Psalm of Praise.*

This Psalm, like the xxv. xxxiv. cxi. cxii. and cxix. is composed in an alphabetical form. It, and the five which follow, consist of pure praises to God, without so much as one complaint or petition. Here (1) David engages himself, and encourages others, to praise God, ver. 1-7, 10, 21. (2) He represents the grounds of praise, viz. The greatness and glory, sovereign power and eternity of God. 1, The greatness and glory of his works, ver. 3-7, 11-18. 2, His unbounded goodness, mercy, and compassion, ver. 7-9; manifested in pitying the afflicted, providing what is necessary for all creatures, and in his readiness to hear and answer his people's prayers, preserve them from evil, and destroy their enemies, ver. 14-20. ¶ In such high praises of God may all my prayers issue at last. And the nearer I approach to mine end, let my heart and lips be the more filled with his praise and honour all the day. While I live on earth, let me publish the heart-engaging wonders of the nature and work of my God.

I'<span class="sc">LL</span> thee extol, my God, O King;
&emsp;I'll bless thy name always.
2 Thee will I bless each day, and will
&emsp;thy name for ever praise.
3 Great is the Lord, much to be prais'd;
&emsp;his greatness search exceeds.
4 Race unto race shall praise thy works,
&emsp;and show thy mighty deeds.

5 I of thy glorious majesty
&emsp;the honour will record;
&emsp;I'll speak of all thy mighty works,
&emsp;which wondrous are, O Lord.
6 Men of thine acts the might shall show,
&emsp;thine acts that dreadful are;
&emsp;And I, thy glory to advance,
&emsp;thy greatness will declare.

7 The mem'ry of thy goodness great
&emsp;they largely shall express;
&emsp;With songs of praise they shall extol
&emsp;thy perfect righteousness.
8 The Lord is very gracious,
&emsp;in him compassions flow;
&emsp;In mercy he is very great,
&emsp;and is to anger slow.

9 The Lord <span class="sc">Jehovah</span> unto all
&emsp;his goodness doth declare;
&emsp;And over all his other works
&emsp;his tender mercies are.
10 Thee all thy works shall praise, O Lord,
&emsp;and thee thy saints shall bless;
11 They shall thy kingdom's glory show,
&emsp;thy pow'r by speech express:

12 To make the sons of men to know
&emsp;his acts done mightily,
&emsp;And of his kingdom th' excellent
&emsp;and glorious majesty.
13 Thy kingdom shall for ever stand,
&emsp;thy reign through ages all.
14 God raiseth all that are bow'd down,
&emsp;upholdeth all that fall.

15 The eyes of all things wait on thee,
&emsp;the giver of all good;
&emsp;And thou, in time convenient,
&emsp;bestow'st on them their food.
16 Thine hand thou open'st lib'rally,
&emsp;and of thy bounty gives
&emsp;Enough to satisfy the need
&emsp;of ev'ry thing that lives.

17 The Lord is just in all his ways,
&emsp;holy in his works all.
18 God's near to all that call on him,
&emsp;in truth that on him call.
19 He will accomplish the desire
&emsp;of those that do him fear:
&emsp;He also will deliver them,
&emsp;and he their cry will hear.

&emsp;that nought can them annoy:

&emsp;will utterly destroy.

&emsp;to publish cease shall never:
&emsp;Let all flesh bless his holy name
&emsp;for ever and for ever.

3 Much to be prais'd, and great God is;

4 Race shall thy works praise unto race,
&emsp;The mighty acts show done by thee.
5 I will speak of the glorious grace,

6 By men the might shall be extoll'd
&emsp;Of all thy dreadful acts, O Lord:

7 They utter shall abundantly
&emsp;The mem'ry of thy goodness great;
&emsp;And shall sing praises cheerfully,
&emsp;Whilst they thy righteousness relate.
8 The Lord our God is gracious,
&emsp;Compassionate is he also;
&emsp;In mercy he is plenteous,
&emsp;But unto wrath and anger slow.

9 Good unto all men is the Lord:
&emsp;O'er all his works his mercy is.
10 Thy works all praise to thee afford:
&emsp;Thy saints, O Lord, thy name shall bless.
11 The glory of thy kingdom show
&emsp;Shall they, and of thy power tell;
12 That so men's sons his deeds may know,
&emsp;His kingdom's grace that doth excel.
13 Thy kingdom hath none end at all,
&emsp;It doth through ages all remain.
14 The Lord upholdeth all that fall,
&emsp;The cast-down raiseth up again.
15 The eyes of all things, Lord, attend,
&emsp;And on thee wait that here do live,
&emsp;And thou, in season due, dost send
&emsp;Sufficient food them to relieve.

16 Yea, thou thine hand dost open wide,
&emsp;And ev'ry thing dost satisfy
&emsp;That lives, and doth on earth abide,
&emsp;Of thy great liberality.
17 The Lord is just in his ways all,
&emsp;And holy in his works each one.
18 He's near to all that on him call,
&emsp;Who call in truth on him alone.

19 God will the just desire fulfil
&emsp;Of such as do him fear and dread:
&emsp;Their cry regard, and hear he will,
&emsp;And save them in the time of need.
20 The Lord preserves all, more and less,
&emsp;That bear to him a loving heart:
&emsp;But workers all of wickedness
&emsp;Destroy will he, and clean subvert.
21 Therefore my mouth and lips I'll frame
&emsp;To speak the praises of the Lord:
&emsp;To magnify his holy name
&emsp;For ever let all flesh accord.

## Psalm CXLVI.

This Psalm contains (1) Pleasant engagements and encouragements to the hearty and constant praises of God, ver. 1, 2, 10. (2) Earnest dissuasives from trusting in man, who is so weak and short-lived, ver. 3, 4. (3) Powerful persuasives to trust in God, whose power and goodness appear so remarkable in creation, providence, and redemption, ver. 5-10. ¶ While I sing, be stirred up, my soul, and all that is within me, to bless his holy name—to depend on him alone, who is my Maker, my faithful Friend, my kind and righteous Protector. my bountiful Provider, my Almighty Deliverer, my gracious Enlightener, my seasonable Restorer, my perpetual Preserver, and the just punisher of my foes;—my King, my God, and mine <span class="sc">all</span>.

P<span class="sc">RAISE</span> God. The Lord praise, O my soul.
2 I'll praise God while I live;
&emsp;While I have being to my God
&emsp;in songs I'll praises give.
3 Trust not in princes, nor man's son,
&emsp;in whom there is no stay:
4 His breath departs, to's earth he turns;
&emsp;that day his thoughts decay.

5 O happy is that man and blest,
&emsp;whom Jacob's God doth aid;
&emsp;Whose hope upon the Lord doth rest,
&emsp;and on his God is stay'd:
6 Who made the earth and heavens high,
&emsp;who made the swelling deep,
&emsp;And all that is within the same;
&emsp;who truth doth ever keep.

7 Who righteous judgment executes
&emsp;for those oppress'd that be,
&emsp;Who to the hungry giveth food;
&emsp;God sets the pris'ners free.
8 The Lord doth give the blind their sight,
&emsp;the bowed down doth raise:
&emsp;The Lord doth dearly love all those
&emsp;that walk in upright ways.
