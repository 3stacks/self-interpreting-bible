37:1 The hand{a} of the LORD was upon me, and carried{b} me out{1} in the Spirit of the LORD, and set me down in the midst of the valley which *was* full of bones,
37:2 And caused me to pass by them round about: and, behold, *there were* very many in the open valley;{2} and, lo, *they were* very {c}dry.
37:3 And he said unto me, Son of man, can these{d} bones live? And I answered, O {e}Lord GOD, thou knowest.{3}
37:4 Again he said unto me, {g}Prophesy upon these bones, and say unto them, O {h}ye dry bones, hear the word of the LORD.{4}
37:5 Thus saith the Lord GOD unto these bones, Behold, {i}I will cause breath to enter into you, and ye shall live:
37:6 And I will lay sinews upon you, and will bring up flesh upon you, and cover you with skin, and put breath in you, and ye shall live; and {j}ye shall know that I *am* the LORD.
37:7 So{k} I prophesied as I was commanded:{5} and as I prophesied {l}there was a noise, and, behold, a shaking, and the bones came together, bone to his bone.
37:8 And when I beheld, lo, the sinews and the flesh came up upon them, and the skin covered them above; but *there was* no {n}breath in them.
37:9 Then said he unto me, Prophesy unto the wind,{6} prophesy, son of man, and say to the wind, Thus saith the Lord GOD, Come from the four{o} winds, O breath,{7} and {p}breathe upon these slain, that they may live.
37:10 So I prophesied as he commanded me, and{q} the breath came into them, and they lived, and stood up upon their feet, an exceeding great army.
37:11 Then he said unto me, Son of man, these bones are the {r}whole house of Israel: behold they say, {s}Our bones are dried, and our hope is lost; we are cut off for our parts.
37:12 Therefore {t}prophesy, and say unto them, Thus saith the Lord GOD, Behold, O my people, {u}I will open your graves, and cause you to come up out of your graves,{8} and {v}bring you into the land of Israel.

37:14 And shall put {y}my Spirit in you, and ye shall live; and I shall place you in your own land: {z}then shall ye know that I the LORD have spoken *it*, and performed *it*, saith the LORD.
37:15 ¶ The word of the LORD came again unto me, saying,
37:16 Moreover, thou son of man, {a}take thee one stick, and write{9} upon it, {b}For Judah, and for the children of Israel his companions: then take another stick, and write upon it, For {c}Joseph, the stick of Ephraim, and *for* all the house of Israel his companions:
37:17 And {d}join them one to another into one stick; and they shall become one in thine hand.
37:18 And when the children of thy people shall speak unto thee, saying, Wilt {e}thou not show us what thou *meanest* by these?
37:19 Say unto them, Thus saith the Lord GOD, Behold, {g}I will take the stick of Joseph, which *is* in the hand of Ephraim, and the tribes of Israel his fellows, and will put them with him, *even* with the stick of Judah, and make them {h}one stick, and they shall be one in mine hand.
37:20 And the sticks whereon thou writest shall be {i}in thine hand before their eyes.

37:23 Neither{n} shall they defile themselves any more with their idols, nor with their detestable things, nor with any of their transgressions: but {o}I will save them out of all their dwelling-places wherein they have sinned, and will {p}cleanse them: {q}so shall they be my people, and I will be their God.
