## Chapter XVIII.
Ver. 1-3. To punish her for corrupting the nations with her idolatry and other wickedness, and enriching her people with her traffic in divine things, the pagan state shall be utterly destroyed, and exposed as a diabolical system of uncleanness, murder, and every other shocking abomination, and Rome shall be turned into a desolate and frightful heap of rubbish. 4-6. As in later times multitudes revolted from paganism, to the advantage of their souls and their countries, so hereafter the faithful followers of Christ ought and shall break off every connection with it, and shall be God's instruments for ruining its power. 7, 8.
