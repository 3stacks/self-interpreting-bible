<!-- illustration: GENERAL VIEW OF THE ERECHTHEUM, ACROPOLIS—THE ERECHTHEUM WAS BURNED BY THE PERSIANS, LED BY DARIUS OR BY AHASUERUS, HIS SON, AND REBUILT IN THE TIME OF PERICLES. [Ezra, vi:1.]—"Then Darius the king made a decree, and search was made in the house of the rolls, where the treasures were made up in Babylon." After the death of Cyrus, Cambyses was made king of Persia. He died of a small wound, and left no issue to succeed him, and his throne was usurped by Darius. Darius burnt Sardis, and thereby so enraged the Athenians that a war was kindled between Greece and Persia. Darius was then so exasperated against the Greeks that a servant every day, by his order, repeated these words: "Remember, O King, to punish the Athenians." He was conquered at the celebrated battle of Marathon by 10,000 Athenians. We give a picture of a general view of the Erechtheum on the Acropolis to illustrate these facts of Grecian history and the connection of Darius with it. It is an interesting fact that the Persians burned down the temple of Erechtheus that stood on this very spot. It was afterwards rebuilt in the time of Pericles. -->
