1:4 Greatly{i} desiring to see thee, being mindful of thy tears, that I may be filled with joy;
1:5 When I call to remembrance the {j}unfeigned faith that is in thee, which dwelt first in thy grandmother Lois, and thy {k}mother Eunice; and I am persuaded that in thee also.
1:6 Wherefore I put thee in remembrance {l}that thou stir up the gift of God, which is in thee by the {n}putting on of my hands.
1:7 For{o} God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.
1:8 Be{p} not thou therefore ashamed of the testimony of our Lord, nor of me his prisoner: but be thou partaker of the afflictions of the gospel according to the power of God;{2}
1:9 Who {q}hath saved us, and called *us* with an holy calling, {r}not according to our works, but according to his own purpose and grace, which was given us in Christ Jesus, before the world began;
1:10 But{s} is now made manifest by the appearing of our Saviour Jesus Christ, who hath abolished{t} death,{3} and hath brought life and immortality to light through the gospel:
1:11 Whereunto{u} I am appointed a preacher, and an apostle, and a teacher of the Gentiles.
1:12 For the which cause I also {v}suffer these things: nevertheless {x}I am not ashamed: for I know whom I have believed,{4} and I am persuaded that {y}he is able to keep that which I have committed{5} unto him against that day.
1:13 Hold{z} fast the form of sound words, which thou hast heard of me, in faith and love which is in Christ Jesus.
1:14 That{a} good thing which was committed unto thee, keep by the Holy Ghost which dwelleth in us.
1:15 This thou knowest, {b}that all they which are in Asia{6} be turned away from me; of whom are Phygellus and Hermogenes.
1:16 The{c} Lord give mercy unto the house of Onesiphorus; for he oft refreshed me, and {d}was not ashamed of my chain.
1:17 But when he was {e}in Rome, he sought me out very diligently, and found *me*.
1:18 The Lord grant unto him {g}that he may find mercy of the Lord in that day: and in how many things he ministered unto me at Ephesus, thou knowest very well.
