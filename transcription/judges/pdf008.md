**Reflections.**—Difficult and extensive is the work which the people of God, and the rulers of nations and churches, have to go through with, after one would think everything was rectified. There is need to acknowledge the Lord in all our ways, that, being animated by him to our proper work, we may prosecute it with courage and success. And the more honour or ability God gives to men, the more difficult work he assigns them. But God most fearfully humbles the proud, marks their sins in their judgments, and metes out to them the measure which they had meted out to others; while such as share with the people of God in their wilderness sufferings, share also in their eternal inheritance.
