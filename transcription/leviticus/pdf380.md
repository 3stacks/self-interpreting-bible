## Chapter VII.
Ver. 13. (1) The prohibition of leaven, in ch. 2.11, related only to things burnt upon the altar; but he did not prohibit the offering of leavened bread for the priest's food. (2) This was a thank-offering to God for blessings, of which leavened bread was one. And it might denote that God rejects not the grateful services of his people, though tainted and mingled with corruption.

Ver. 15. This intimated that Christ and his grace should be received without delay, and with thanksgiving speedily returned; and it also prompted them to invite the poor, or strangers, to share in the entertainment.
