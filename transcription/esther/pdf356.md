Ver. 6. [The open courts of all eastern houses of the better class have tesselated pavement of marble. Some of those in Damascus are exceedingly rich and beautiful. The divans too are covered with cloth of gold or silver, with heavy fringe of gold. *P.*]

Ver. 13. [This expression appears to include both statute and common law; or, in other words, the law as written, and the ordinary practice followed in court. It is worthy of note that Ahasuerus, though a fickle and passionate tyrant, here thinks it right to consult his ministers. He threw upon them the responsibility. *P.*]
