## Chapter XV.
[Ver. 2. *Abishalom* is a lengthened form of Absalom, and the person alluded to is most probably the well-known son of king David. His daughter Thamar appears to have married Uriel of Gibeah, and bare him Maachah, who was the mother of Abijam. This illustrious descent will account for the mention of the grandfather rather than the father. *P.*]
