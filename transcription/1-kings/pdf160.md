[I KINGS, ix: 11.]—(Now Hiram, king of Tyre, had furnished Solomon with cedar trees and fir trees, and with gold, according to all his desire), that then King Solomon gave Hiram twenty cities in the land of Galilee." As illustrative of this Scripture we give a picture of a view in Lebanon. Lebanon Mountains, we are told, Hiram, King of Tyre, secured the cedars which he sent by way of Joppa to Solomon for use in building the temple. The Lebanon Mountains have been called the focus of Syria. In these mountains arise four great rivers The Orontes flows northward, making Antioch possible; the Abana flows east, creating Damascus; the Litany flows west, and the Jordan flows south. Of these rivers only two reach the open sea—the Litany and the Orontes. The Jordan ends in the Dead Sea, and the Abana sinks out of sight in the Syrian desert. The Lebanon Mountains have always been celebrated for the great cedar trees which grow in them. -->
