It is about 200 miles from this particular source of the Jordan which flows from the roots of Anti-Lebanon to the Dead Sea, about 136 miles in a straight line. It is the river of the great plain of Palestine. The view we get here of the source of the Jordan is in the ancient city of Cæsarea Philippi. Frequent mention is made of the River Jordan in the Scripture, as a boundary. It was the limit of the Holy Land on the eastern side. The Jordan has several sources near Cæsarea Philippi. It passes through the lakes of Merom and Galilee. Two remarkable features about the Jordan are its descent and its windings. Between the Lake Galilee and the Dead Sea there are 2 rapids. At Lake Galilee the river is 653 feet beneath sea level, and at the Dead Sea it is 1316 feet beneath sea level. -->
