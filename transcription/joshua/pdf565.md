## Chapter XI.
[Ver. 1. We have here another confederacy against Joshua, formed by the kings to the *north* of Jerusalem.—*Note*, This succession of confederacies is no more than what occurs to all who follow the true *Joshua* to the true promised land. One trouble is no sooner forgot than another succeeds; and one temptation is no sooner resisted and subdued, than another tempter, like Satan with our Lord, Mat. iv., tries some new scheme to pervert our principles and overcome our resolutions. *C.*]

Ver. 4. [*Horses and chariots very many.* This is the first time that *horses* are mentioned since the exodus. *Camels, asses*, and *sheep*, during the intermediate period, are frequently enumerated, but the *horse* never.
