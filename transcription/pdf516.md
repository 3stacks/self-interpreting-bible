10 From hands of those that hated them
&emsp;he did his people save;
And from the en'my's cruel hand
&emsp;to them redemption gave.

11 The waters overwhelm'd their foes;
&emsp;not one was left alive.

12 Then they believ'd his word, and praise
&emsp;to him in songs did give.

13 But soon did they his mighty works
&emsp;forget unthankfully,
And on his counsel and his will
&emsp;did not wait patiently;

14 But much did lust in wilderness,
&emsp;and God in desert tempt.

15 He gave them what they sought, but to
&emsp;their soul he leanness sent.

16 And against Moses in the camp
&emsp;their envy did appear;
At Aaron they, the saint of God,
&emsp;envious also were.

17 Therefore the earth did open wide,
&emsp;and Dathan did devour,
And all Abiram's company
&emsp;did cover in that hour.

18 Likewise among their company
&emsp;a fire was kindled then;
And so the hot consuming flame
&emsp;burnt up these wicked men.

19 Upon the hill of Horeb they
&emsp;an idol-calf did frame,
A molten image they did make,
&emsp;and worshipped the same.

20 And thus their glory, and their God,
&emsp;most vainly changed they
Into the likeness of an ox
&emsp;that eateth grass or hay.

21 They did forget the mighty God,
&emsp;that had their Saviour been,
By whom such great things brought to pass
&emsp;they had in Egypt seen.

22 In Ham's land he did wondrous works,
&emsp;things terrible did he,
When he his mighty hand and arm
&emsp;stretch'd out at the Red sea.

23 Then said he, He would them destroy,
&emsp;had not, his wrath to stay,
His chosen Moses stood in breach,
&emsp;that them he should not slay.

24 Yea, they despis'd the pleasant land,
&emsp;believed not his word:

25 But in their tents they murmured,
&emsp;not heark'ning to the Lord.

26 Therefore in desert them to slay
&emsp;he lifted up his hand:

27 'Mong nations to o'erthrow their seed,
&emsp;and scatter in each land.

28 They unto Baal-peor did
&emsp;themselves associate;
The sacrifices of the dead
&emsp;they did profanely eat.

29 Thus, by their lewd inventions,
&emsp;they did provoke his ire;
And then upon them suddenly
&emsp;the plague brake in as fire.

30 Then Phin'has rose, and justice did,
&emsp;and so the plague did cease;

31 That to all ages counted was
&emsp;to him for righteousness.

32 And at the waters, where they strove,
&emsp;they did him angry make,
In such sort, that it fared ill
&emsp;with Moses for their sake:

33 Because they there his spirit meek
&emsp;provoked bitterly,
So that he utter'd with his lips
&emsp;words unadvisedly.

34 Nor, as the Lord commanded them,
&emsp;did they the nations slay:

35 But with the heathen mingled were,
&emsp;and learn'd of them their way.

36 And they their idols serv'd, which did
&emsp;a snare unto them turn.

37 Their sons and daughters they to dev'ls
&emsp;in sacrifice did burn.

38 In their own children's guiltless blood
&emsp;their hands they did imbrue,
Whom to Canaan's idols they
&emsp;for sacrifices slew:
So was the land defil'd with blood.
39 They stain'd with their own way,
And with their own inventions
&emsp;a whoring they did stray.

40 Against his people kindled was
&emsp;the wrath of God therefore,
Insomuch that he did his own
&emsp;inheritance abhor.

41 He gave them to the heathen's hand;
&emsp;their foes did them command.

42 Their en'mies them oppress'd, they were
&emsp;made subject to their hand.

43 He many times deliver'd them;
&emsp;but with their counsel so
They him provok'd, that for their sin
&emsp;they were brought very low.

44 Yet their affliction he beheld,
&emsp;when he did hear their cry:

45 And he for them his covenant
&emsp;did call to memory;
After his mercies' multitude
46 he did repent: And made
Them to be pity'd of all those
&emsp;who did them captive lead.

47 O Lord our God, us save, and gather
&emsp;the heathen from among,
That we thy holy name may praise
&emsp;in a triumphant song.

48 Bless'd be <span class="sc">Jehovah</span>, Isr'el's God,
&emsp;to all eternity:
Let all the people say, Amen.
&emsp;Praise to the Lord give ye.

## Psalm CVII.

This Psalm may be considered as an appendix to the two foregoing. Those represented the providence of God towards his chosen Israelites, who prefigured his gospel church: this (1) Represents the providences of God to the children of men in general, and calls them to praise him for his kindness and mercy therein: particularly, 1. In directing and gathering travellers whom he had justly dispersed, ver. 1-7. 2. In delivering prisoners whom he had justly confined, ver. 8-14. 3. In healing the sick whom he had justly plagued, ver. 15-22. 4. In relieving mariners whom he had justly brought into a storm, ver. 23-30. 5. In changing the condition of territories, persons, and families at his pleasure, to the comfort of his people, and the restraining of wickedness, ver. 31-42. (2) It encourages men to a careful observation of God's providences, ver. 43. ¶ Let me sing this with a heart deeply affected with the various providences of God towards myself and others, while we have walked by the way; been confined in prisons; or lain in beds of languishing; traversed the surface of the depths, or laboured in our respective employments. Let me be a careful observer of providences, and compare them with the oracles of God, with my behaviour and my prayers, and with one another. So shall I understand the loving-kindness of the Lord.

P<span class="sc">RAISE</span> God, for he is good: for still
&emsp;his mercies lasting be.

2 Let God's redeem'd say so, whom he
&emsp;from th' en'my's hand did free;

3 And gather'd them out of the lands,
&emsp;from north, south, east, and west.

4 They stray'd in desert's pathless way,
&emsp;no city found to rest.

5 For thirst and hunger in them faints

6 their soul. When straits them press,
They cry unto the Lord, and he
&emsp;them frees from their distress.

7 Them also in a way to walk
&emsp;that right is he did guide,
That they might to a city go,
&emsp;wherein they might abide.

8 O that men to the Lord would give
&emsp;praise for his goodness then,
And for his works of wonder done
&emsp;unto the sons of men!

9 For he the soul that longing is
&emsp;doth fully satisfy;
With goodness he the hungry soul
&emsp;doth fill abundantly.

10 Such as shut up in darkness deep,
&emsp;and in death's shade abide,
Whom strongly hath affliction bound,
&emsp;and irons fast have ty'd:

11 Because against the words of God
&emsp;they wrought rebelliously,
And they the counsel did contemn
&emsp;of him that is most High:

12 Their heart he did bring down with grief,
&emsp;they fell, no help could have.

13 In trouble then they cry'd to God,
&emsp;he them from straits did save.

14 He out of darkness did them bring,
&emsp;and from death's shade them take;
These bands, wherewith they had been bound,
&emsp;asunder quite he brake.

15 O that men to the Lord would give
&emsp;praise for his goodness then,
And for his works of wonder done
&emsp;unto the sons of men!

16 Because the mighty gates of brass
&emsp;in pieces he did tear,
By him in sunder also cut
&emsp;the bars of iron were.

17 Fools, for their sin, and their offence,
&emsp;do sore affliction bear;

18 All kind of meat their soul abhors;
&emsp;they to death's gates draw near.

19 In grief they cry to God; he saves
&emsp;them from their miseries.

20 He sends his word, them heals, and them
&emsp;from their destructions frees.

21 O that men to the Lord would give
&emsp;praise for his goodness then,
And for his works of wonder done
&emsp;unto the sons of men!

22 And let them sacrifice to him
&emsp;off'rings of thankfulness;
And let them show abroad his works
&emsp;in songs of joyfulness.

23 Who go to sea in ships, and in
&emsp;great waters trading be,

24 Within the deep these men God's works
&emsp;and his great wonders see.

25 For he commands, and forth in haste
&emsp;the stormy tempest flies,
Which makes the sea with rolling waves
&emsp;aloft to swell and rise.

26 They mount to heav'n, then to the depths
&emsp;they do go down again;
Their soul doth faint and melt away
&emsp;with trouble and with pain.

27 They reel and stagger like one drunk,
&emsp;at their wit's end they be:

28 Then they to God in trouble cry,
&emsp;who them from straits doth free.

29 The storm is chang'd into a calm
&emsp;at his command and will;
So that the waves, which rag'd before,
&emsp;now quiet are and still.

30 Then are they glad, because at rest
&emsp;and quiet now they be:
So to the haven he them brings,
&emsp;which they desir'd to see.

31 O that men to the Lord would give
&emsp;praise for his goodness then,
And for his works of wonder done
&emsp;unto the sons of men!

32 Among the people gathered
&emsp;let them exalt his name;
Among assembled elders spread
&emsp;his most renowned fame.

33 He to dry land turns water-springs,
&emsp;and floods to wilderness;

34 For sins of those that dwell therein,
&emsp;fat land to barrenness.
