7:3 And when I was born, I drew in the common air, and fell upon the earth, which is of like nature, and the first voice which I uttered was crying, as all others do.
7:4 I was nursed in {3}swaddling-clothes, and that with cares.
7:5 For there is no king that had any other beginning of birth.
7:6 {4}For all men have one entrance into life, and the like going out.
7:7 {5}Wherefore I prayed, and understanding was given me: I called *upon God*, and the spirit of wisdom came to me.
7:8 I preferred her before sceptres and thrones, and esteemed riches nothing in comparison of her.
7:9 Neither compared I unto her any {6}precious stone, because all gold in respect of her is as a little sand, and silver shall be counted as clay before her.
7:10 I loved her above health and beauty, and chose to have her instead of light: for the light that cometh from her never goeth out.
7:11 {7}All good things together came to me with her, and innumerable riches in her hands.
7:12 And I rejoiced in *them* all, because wisdom goeth before them: and I knew not that she was the mother of them.
7:13 I learned {8}diligently, and do communicate *her* {9}liberally: I do not hide her riches.
7:14 For she is a treasure unto men, that never faileth: which they that {*}use become the friends of God, being commended for the gifts that come from learning.
7:15 {†}God hath granted me to speak as I would, and to conceive as is meet for the things that {‡}are given me: because it is he that leadeth unto wisdom, and directeth the wise.
7:16 For in his hand are both we and our words; {||}all wisdom also, and knowledge of workmanship.
7:17 For he hath given me certain knowledge of the things that are, namely, to know how {1}the world was made, and the operation of the elements:
7:18 The beginning, ending, and midst of the times: the alterations of the turnings *of the sun*, and {2}the change of seasons:
7:19 The {3}circuits of years, and the positions of stars:
7:20 The natures of living creatures, and the furies of wild beasts: the violence of winds, and the reasonings of men: the diversities of plants, and the virtues of roots:
7:21 And all such things as are {4}either secret or manifest, them I know.
7:22 For wisdom, which is the worker of all things, taught me: for in her is an understanding spirit, holy, {5}one only, manifold, subtil, lively, clear, undefiled, plain, not subject to hurt, loving the thing that is good, quick, which cannot be letted, ready to do good,
7:23 {6}Kind to man, steadfast, sure, free from care, having all power, overseeing all things, and going through all understanding, pure and most subtil spirits.
7:24 For wisdom is more moving than any motion: she passeth and goeth through all things by reason of her pureness.
7:25 For she is the {7}breath of the power of God, and a pure {8}influence flowing from the glory of the Almighty: therefore can no defiled thing fall into her.
7:26 For she is the {9}brightness of the everlasting light, the unspotted mirror of the power of God, and {*}the image of his goodness.
7:27 And being but one, she can do all things: and remaining in herself, she {†}maketh all things new: and in all ages entering into holy souls, she maketh them friends of God, and prophets.
7:28 {‡}For God loveth none but him that dwelleth with wisdom.
7:29 For she is more beautiful than the sun, and above all the order of stars: being compared with the light, she is found before it.
7:30 For after this cometh night: but vice shall not prevail against wisdom.

2 He is in love with wisdom: for he that hath it, hath every good thing. 21 It cannot be had but from God.

<!-- text -->
8:1 Wisdom reacheth from one end to another mightily: and {1}sweetly doth she order all things.
8:2 I loved her, {2}and sought *her* out from my youth: I desired {3}to make *her* my spouse, and I was a lover of her beauty.
8:3 In that she is conversant with God, she magnifieth her nobility: yea, {4}the Lord of all things himself loved her.
8:4 For she is {5}privy to the mysteries of the knowledge of God, and a {6}lover of his works.
8:5 If riches be a possession to be desired in this life; {7}what is richer than wisdom, that worketh all things?
8:6 And if {8}prudence work; who of all that are, is a more cunning workman than she?
8:7 And if a man love righteousness, her labours are {9}virtues, for she teacheth temperance and prudence, justice and fortitude: which are such things as men can have nothing more profitable in their life.
8:8 If a man desire much {*}experience, she knoweth things of old, and conjectureth *aright* what is to come: she knoweth the subtilties of speeches, and can expound dark sentences: she foreseeth signs and wonders, and the events of {†}seasons and times.
8:9 Therefore I purposed to take her to me to live with me, knowing that she {‡}would be a counsellor of good things, and a comfort in cares and grief.
8:10 For her sake I shall have estimation among the multitude, and honour with the {||}elders, though I be young.
8:11 I shall be found of a quick conceit in judgment, and shall be admired in the sight of great men.
8:12 {1}When I hold my tongue, they shall abide my leisure, and when I speak, they shall give good ear unto me: if I talk much, they shall {2}lay their hands upon their mouth.
8:13 Moreover by the means of her I shall obtain immortality, and leave behind me an everlasting memorial to them that come after me.
8:14 I shall {3}set the people in order, and the nations shall be subject unto me.
8:15 Horrible tyrants shall be afraid when they do but hear of me: I shall {4}be found good among the multitude, and valiant in war.
8:16 {5}After I have come into my house, I will repose myself with her: for her conversation hath no bitterness; and to live with her hath no sorrow, but mirth and joy.
8:17 Now, when I considered these things in myself, and {6}pondered them in my heart, how that to be allied unto wisdom is immortality;
8:18 And great pleasure it is to have her friendship; and in the works of her hands are {7}infinite riches; and in the exercise of conference with her, prudence; and in talking with her, {8}a good report; I went about seeking how to {9}take her to me.
8:19 For I was a witty child, and had a good spirit.
8:20 Yea rather, being good, I came into a body undefiled.
8:21 Nevertheless, when I perceived that I could not otherwise obtain her, {*}except God gave her me; and that was a point of wisdom also to know whose gift she was; I {†}prayed unto the Lord, and besought him, and with {‡}my whole heart I said.

<!-- chapter: 9 -->
<!-- summary -->
A prayer unto God for his wisdom, without which the best man is nothing worth, neither can he tell how to please God.

<!-- text -->
9:1 O God of my fathers, and Lord of mercy, {1}who hast made all things with thy word.
9:2 And ordained man through thy wisdom, that he should {2}have dominion over the creatures which thou hast made,
9:3 And order the world according to equity and righteousness, and execute judgment with an upright heart:
9:4 Give {3}me wisdom, that sitteth by thy throne; and reject me not from among thy children:
9:5 For I {4}thy servant, and son of thy handmaid, am a feeble person, and of a short time, and too young for the understanding of judgment and laws.
9:6 For though a man be never so perfect among the children of men, yet if thy wisdom be not with him, he shall be nothing regarded.
9:7 Thou hast chosen me to be a {5}king of thy people, and a judge of thy sons and daughters:
9:8 Thou hast commanded me to {6}build a temple upon thy holy mount, and an altar in the city wherein thou dwellest, a resemblance of the holy tabernacle, which thou hast prepared from the beginning.
9:9 And {7}wisdom was with thee: which knoweth thy works, {8}and was present when thou madest the world, and knew what was acceptable in thy sight, and right in thy commandments.
