# The Third Epistle of John.

'Gaius' (in Latin, Caius) is a name occurring on four other occasions, viz. Ac. 19. 29; 20. 4; Ro. 16. 23; 1 Co. 1. 14. Who this Gaius was, or where he resided, is unknown;—but it matters not. His character survives, that, in these latter days, Christians may learn what primitive Christians were. *C.*
