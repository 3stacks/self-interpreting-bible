[Matthew, xiv:2.]—Samaria is the capital built by Omri, the King of Israel, which suffered like Shechem, both being pillaged by John Hyrcanus. It was restored by Gabinius, but nothing remains of the ancient city now. There are many pillars and columns forming a colonnade around the brow of the hill upon which the ancient city was built, but these date from the time of Herod, and are a part of the remains of the temple he built in honor of Augustus. The Church of St. John it is claimed was built over the place where John the Baptist was beheaded. For a long time, it is contended, John was a prisoner here. The weight of historic opinion sustains the claim that this is the identical place where he was beheaded. -->
