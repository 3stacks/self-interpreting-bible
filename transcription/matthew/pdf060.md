10:2+ these: {c}The first, Simon,{2} who is called Peter, and Andrew his brother; James *the son* of Zebedee, and John his brother;
10:3 Philip,{d} and Bartholomew; Thomas, and Matthew the publican; {e}James *the son* of Alphaeus, and Lebbaeus, whose surname was Thaddaeus;{3}
10:4 Simon {4}the Canaanite,{5} and {g}Judas Iscariot,{6} who also betrayed him.
10:5 ¶ These twelve Jesus sent forth, and commanded them, saying, {h}Go not into the way of the Gentiles, and into *any* city of the Samaritans enter ye not:{7}
10:6 But{i} go rather to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.
10:7 And, as ye go, {j}preach, saying, The kingdom of heaven is at hand.
10:8 Heal the sick, cleanse the lepers, raise the dead, cast out devils: freely ye have received, freely give.
10:9 Provide{8} neither gold, nor silver, nor brass, in your purses;
10:10 Nor scrip for *your* journey, neither two coats, neither shoes, nor yet staves;{9} for{k} the workman is worthy of his meat.
10:11 And into whatsoever city or town ye shall enter, inquire who in it is worthy;{1} and there abide till ye go thence.
10:12 And when ye come into an house, {l}salute it.
10:13 And if the house be worthy, let your peace come upon it:{2} but if it be not worthy, let your peace return{3} to you.
10:14 And{n} whosoever shall not receive you, nor hear your words, when ye depart out of that house or city, shake off the dust of your feet.
10:15 Verily I say unto you, {o}It shall be more tolerable for the land of Sodom and Gomorrah{4} in the day of judgment, than for that city.
10:16 ¶ Behold,{p} I send you forth as sheep in the midst of wolves: be ye therefore wise as serpents, and harmless{5} as doves.
10:17 But{q} beware of men: for they will deliver you up to the councils, and they will scourge you in their synagogues;
10:18 And ye shall be brought before governors and kings for my sake, for a testimony against them and the Gentiles.
10:19 But{r} when they deliver you up, take no thought how or what ye shall speak; for it shall be given you in that same hour what ye shall speak.
10:20 For it is not ye that speak, but the Spirit of your Father which speaketh in you.{6}
10:21 And{s} the brother shall deliver up the brother to death, and the father the child: and the children shall rise up against *their* parents, and cause them to be put to death.
10:22 And{t} ye shall be hated of all *men* for my name's sake; {u}but he that endureth to the end shall be saved.
10:23 But{v} when they persecute you in this city, flee ye into another:{7} for verily I say unto you, Ye shall not have gone over{8} the cities of Israel, till the Son of man be {9}come.{1}
10:24 The{x} disciple is not above *his* master, nor the servant above his lord.
10:25 It is enough for the disciple that he be as his master, and the servant as his lord. {y}If they have called the master of the house {2}Beelzebub,{3} how much more *shall they call* them of his household?
10:26 Fear{z} them not{4} therefore: {a}for there is nothing covered, that shall not be revealed; and hid, that shall not be known.
10:27 What I tell you in darkness, *that* speak ye in light: and what ye hear in the ear, *that* preach ye upon the {5}house-tops.{6}
10:28 And{b} fear not them which kill the body, but are not able to kill the soul: but rather fear him which is able to destroy both soul and body in hell.
10:29 Are{c} not two sparrows sold for a {7}farthing? and one of them shall not fall on the ground without your Father.
10:30 But{d} the very hairs of your head are all numbered.
10:31 Fear{e} ye not therefore; ye are of more value than many sparrows.
10:32 Whosoever{g} therefore shall confess me before men, him will I confess also before my Father which is in heaven.
10:33 But{h} whosoever shall deny me before men, him will I also deny before my Father which is in heaven.
10:34 Think{i} not that I am come to send peace on earth: I came not to send peace, but a sword.
10:35 For{j} I am come to set a man at variance against his father, and the daughter against her mother, and the daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law.
10:36 And a man's foes *shall be* they of his own household.
10:37 He{k} that loveth father or mother more than me, is not worthy of me: and he that loveth son or daughter more than me, is not worthy of me.
