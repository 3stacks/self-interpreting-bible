# Self-Interpreting Bible: page map of Vol. II, III and IV

All numbers are PDF page numbers. "top" means that the book title is at the top of the page.
"part-way" means that the page also holds the end of the previous item. Most books end with a
"Concluding remarks on the book of X" section. That section is part of the book range.
A plate is a full-page engraving or map with no running head. Plates inside a book range are
listed only under "plates".

## Method and checks
- Each page was examined as a thumbnail (5 percent) and as a running-head crop. Book starts that were not clear were examined at 15-18 percent.
- Plate lists were checked two ways: pages with an empty running-head crop, and pages with dark thumbnails. The only unlisted dark page is Vol. III 544 (decorative family record form).
- The Vol. IV contents page prints each page number one line below its entry. The Revelation line shows 510, and 513 (the true start of Revelation) is on the next line, beside a stamp.
